### **B) Liquidity risk**

The Company’s principal source of liquidity are ‘cash and cash equivalents’ and cash flows that are generated from operations. The Company believes that its working capital is sufficient to meet the financial liabilities within maturity period. The Company has no outstanding long-term borrowings except sales tax deferral liability amounting to ₹ 73.30 crore which are interest free and are repayable after 7 years from the Balance Sheet date. Additionally, the Company has invested its surplus funds in fixed income securities or instruments of similar profile thereby ensuring safety of capital and availability of liquidity as and when required. Hence the Company carries a negligible liquidity risk.
