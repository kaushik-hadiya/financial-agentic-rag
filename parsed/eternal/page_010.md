## **B. Quick commerce**

image: illustration of a Blinkit delivery partner handing a bag to a customer

Blinkit is a quick commerce B2C platform providing on-demand delivery of products across multiple categories. Customers can place orders on the Blinkit app and have them delivered to their doorstep. Orders are fulfilled through a network of stores located typically within a 3 km radius from the customer and are delivered by a last mile delivery fleet of independent delivery partners. Blinkit offers a wide assortment including daily essentials, electronics, beauty & personal care, home décor, toys & games, sports accessories, general merchandise and more.

In FY26, we transitioned the quick commerce business from a marketplace model to inventory ownership. Owning inventory gives us greater control over assortment depth, margins and lets us expand assortment faster, making our business more durable over time. By Mar-26, ~90% of our quick commerce NOV was on our own inventory, which we expect to be the steady state going forward.

Blinkit continued to expand its geographical footprint during the fiscal and added 942 net new stores to its network. We also expanded our backend warehousing infrastructure to support this growth. As of Mar-26, Blinkit managed a total of 8.5 million sqft. of warehousing space.
