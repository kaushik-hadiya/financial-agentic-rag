Quick commerce Revenue grew 626% YoY due to shift to inventory ownership where Revenue now includes the full monetary value of goods sold (and not just the marketplace commission).

Since Q3FY26, ~90% of NOV was transacted on our own inventory, which we expect to be the steady state level. Accordingly, FY26 quick commerce Revenue is not comparable with previous years.

Gross profit (like-for-like revenue) grew 144% YoY in FY26, faster than NOV growth.

*Note: Gross Profit is calculated as quick commerce Revenue (-) cost of goods sold in case of own inventory sales.*
