## **10. Health and safety management system:**

### **a. Whether an occupational health and safety management system has been implemented by the entity? (Yes/ No). If yes, the coverage of such a system?**

Yes. At Eternal, our Health and Safety Policy includes nine pillars that outline how we effectively manage critical Occupational Health and Safety (OH&S) aspects. Additionally, we hold the ISO 45001:2018 certification for our OH&S Management System. The Audit Committee of Eternal Limited maintains oversight on health and safety related matters and reviews them on a quarterly basis.

The Environment, Health and Safety (EHS) team helps the organization maintain high levels of safety standards. Our offices are equipped with essential safety systems such as fire detection, fire fighting mechanisms, public address systems, safety signage, etc., to ensure the safety of employees and workers. Additionally, there is a Safety Reporting System (SRS) that enables employees and workers to report OH&S incidents and hazards actively. The system ensures seamless tracking, analysis and resolution, thereby building a proactive safety culture and enabling continuous improvement of our OH&S management system.

Health and Safety training is mandatory for all employees of Eternal and a key pillar of our Health & Safety policy. In FY26, we strengthened our safety culture through a series of focused awareness initiatives. We marked National Road Safety month, with a mix of on-ground and digital engagement activities, including interactive carnivals, recognition of road safety champions under the company-wide “Sadak Suraksha Jeevan Raksha” campaign. During this month, we also conducted engaging awareness initiatives across offices, emphasizing people safety and SRS reporting, while reinforcing the message that “safety is a habit we build together.” These efforts are further supported by continuous monitoring by the Workplace & Facilities team to ensure sustained adherence to safety standards.

### **b. What are the processes used to identify work-related hazards and assess risks on a routine and non-routine basis by the entity?**

Risk identification and mitigation remain integral to our Health and Safety policy. We adopt a proactive approach to identifying, assessing and mitigating hazards across both routine and non-routine activities. An integrated action plan is also in place to effectively respond to emergency situations.

As part of our ISO 45001:2018-certified Occupational Health and Safety Management System, we follow documented procedures to systematically evaluate work-related hazards and risks across all operations. Employees and workers are encouraged to report hazards, near-misses and incidents through our Safety Reporting System (SRS), enabling structured investigation and timely closure of such cases.

We continuously strengthen our approach through regular internal inspections and audits to proactively identify and address workplace risks. Identified risks are managed through structured action plans with defined timelines and measurable targets to ensure effective mitigation.

### **c. Whether you have processes for workers to report the work-related hazards and to remove themselves from such risks. (Y/ N)**

Yes, we have established a robust process for workers to report work-related hazards and protect themselves from associated risks. In FY24, we introduced the Safety Reporting System (SRS), enabling employees and workers to report health and safety hazards, incidents, and near-misses.

Building on this, the process is now well established across the organization. During FY26, we further strengthened its implementation through multiple awareness sessions, townhalls and training programs, ensuring it remains a reliable and accessible platform. We also ensured timely closure of identified risks, supported by effective Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and implementation of Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA).
