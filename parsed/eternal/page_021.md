## 4 **Adjusted EBITDA margin (% of NOV) expanded to 5.3% during FY26**

Food delivery Adjusted EBITDA grew 34% YoY to INR 2,017 crore in FY26. Adjusted EBITDA margin improved from 4.6% in FY25 to 5.3% in FY26.

Margin improvement was largely driven by increase in revenue per order and cost efficiencies even as the order mix shifted towards lower-value orders.
