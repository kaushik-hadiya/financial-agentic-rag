### **Beyond our value chain**

Even as a young company, we have tried to give back to society through initiatives that sit outside our value chain. Feeding India (FI), is an example of that.

Daily feeding program by fi

Feeding India currently serves over 1.4 lakh children daily across 2,300+ schools and Anganwadis and has served ~300 million meals since inception. In FY26, FI leveraged technology to build higher trust with donors. A mobile application verifies each child through facial recognition attendance and validates each meal as it is received, ensuring every donated rupee translates into a verified meal. Donors can search 'Feeding India' on Zomato and Blinkit apps to see the number of meals their contributions have funded.

In FY26, FI also extended its effort beyond providing meals to improving the spaces where those meals are served. Anganwadis serve as the first point of contact between India's underprivileged children and the public care system, yet many operate out of rooms that were never built for the purpose. In FY26, FI rebuilt 45 of them across Varanasi, Nandurbar, Gurugram and Kushinagar into dedicated care centres. These centres are safe, functional spaces where children can be served meals, taught and looked after the way the system intends. Separately, when floods struck Punjab in September 2025, FI worked with local NGO partners to distribute 5,250 relief kits to the affected families.

We also scaled the Blinkit Ambulance service during the fiscal year. In FY26, this service provided emergency medical assistance in 10,000+ cases.
