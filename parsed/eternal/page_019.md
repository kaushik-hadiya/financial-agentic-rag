Food delivery NOV grew 16% YoY to INR 37,993 crore in FY26, driven by 15% YoY growth in Orders while NAOV remained broadly flat (0.7% YoY growth). Order volume growth was largely driven by growth in average monthly transacting customers (MTC), which grew 18% YoY to 24.3 million in FY26.

NOV growth recovered progressively through the year after bottoming out in Q1FY26, with Q4FY26 NOV growth accelerating to 19% YoY. The recovery was driven by an improvement in the overall demand environment and our continued focus on expanding the addressable market into more price-sensitive segments. During FY26, we lowered the minimum order value for free delivery under the Gold program from INR 199 to INR 99 and stepped up targeted customer activation.

We expect YoY NOV growth to continue trending towards our long-term expectation of 20%+ over time, driven by gradual market share gains and the compounding impact of continued investments in affordability and selection.
