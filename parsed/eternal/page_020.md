## 3 **Adjusted Revenue grew faster than NOV**

Adjusted Revenue grew 24% YoY to INR 11,698 crore in FY26.

Adjusted Revenue as a % of NOV improved to 30.8% in FY26 compared to 28.7% in FY25 largely driven by better ad monetization and increase in platform fee.

These gains more than offset the reduction in customer delivery charge per order due to the free delivery benefit available on Gold orders.
