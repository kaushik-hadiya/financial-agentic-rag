Blinkit added 942 net new stores in FY26, taking the total count to 2,243 stores across 250+ cities as of Mar-26. Store additions were driven by expansion into new localities and increasing network density in existing areas where demand was exceeding capacity.

To support this rapid expansion, we added ~3.3 million sqft. of backend warehousing space during FY26, taking the total warehousing footprint to 8.5 million sqft. as of Mar-26. We remain on track to achieve our target of ~3,000 stores by Mar-27.

Despite the accelerated pace of store additions, average NOV per day, per store remained broadly stable at INR 756k in FY26.
