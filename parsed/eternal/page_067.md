In case there are any changes made to these settings, the consumer is notified about the same.

* **Print Store:** Enables consumers to upload documents, passport-sized photos and pictures for printing and have them delivered within minutes through the Blinkit app, making it a convenient solution for last-minute needs.

* **Pharma Category:** Blinkit deployed a seamless, teleconsultation and prescription verification workflow. Under this framework, customer-uploaded prescriptions undergo structured pharmacist review, with automated escalation to certified physicians for secondary verification if required. For unprescribed orders, the app instantly connects users to certified doctors for on-demand consultations and e-prescription issuance.
