### **(c) Financial risk management**

#### **Financial risk factors**

The Group’s activities exposes it to a variety of financial risks namely market risk, credit risk and liquidity risk. The Group’s primary focus is to foresee the unpredictability of financial markets and seek to minimise potential adverse effects on its financial performance.

Risk management is carried out by senior management for cash and cash equivalents, trade receivables, investments, deposits with banks and NBFC, foreign currency risk exposure and liquidity risk.
