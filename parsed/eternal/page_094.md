## **26. Overview of the entity’s material responsible business conduct issues**

In FY26, we conducted a double materiality assessment with an independent external organization to re-assess our sustainability priorities in line with evolving business needs and stakeholder expectations. The assessment evaluated sustainability issues through the dual lens of impact and financial materiality, enabling us to understand both the impact our business has on people and the environment, as well as the potential implications of sustainability-related issues on our business performance and long-term value creation. The outcome of the exercise for Eternal Limited on a standalone basis is reflected in the list of material topics below organized under Environment, Social and Governance categories.
