## **<u>Principle 9</u> – Businesses should engage with and provide value to their consumers in a responsible manner**

### **Essential Indicators**

#### **1. Describe the mechanisms in place to receive and respond to consumer complaints and feedback**

We have a system to collect queries and feedback from our consumers through multiple channels including the customer support section within the Zomato app, social media channels, email, app ratings and reviews. Additionally, after every supported chat, feedback is sought to gauge their satisfaction with the support received. Trends derived from the analysis of the feedback and ratings are used to implement corrective measures as required.

If the consumers complaints are still unresolved they can also reach out/ escalate through the below channels -

* <u>grievance@eternal.com</u>
* <u>nodal@eternal.com</u>

Eternal introduced Nugget, an AI-native customer support platform that handles and resolves customer grievances autonomously. This has resulted in faster query resolution, personalized support, improved customer satisfaction and delivered significant cost savings. Nugget is also available to businesses outside of Eternal, enabling them to enhance their customer support operations effortlessly.
