Improvement in Adjusted EBITDA was largely driven by improvement in food delivery Adjusted EBITDA margin which was partly offset by investments in quick commerce, going-out and Others segments. We expect to reach $1 billion of consolidated Adjusted EBITDA by FY29.

Consolidated EBITDA grew 90% YoY to INR 1,208 crore in FY26 from INR 637 crore in FY25. EBITDA is computed as Adjusted EBITDA less share-based payment expense plus rental paid for the period pertaining to ‘Ind AS 116 leases’. EBITDA grew faster than Adjusted EBITDA as ESOP expense remained flattish with only 3% YoY increase, while the rent pertaining to ‘Ind AS 116 leases’ grew rapidly in line with the growth in our quick commerce business.

Profit after tax (PAT) declined to INR 366 crore in FY26 from INR 527 crore in FY25 despite YoY increase in consolidated Adjusted EBITDA. Decrease in PAT was mainly due to increase in depreciation & amortisation expense in the quick commerce business.
