## **8. Does the entity have any project related to reducing greenhouse gas emission? If Yes, then provide details.**

Yes, below are the details of our projects-

* **Project 1: Carbon market instruments**
    We have maintained 100% of our Scope 1 & Scope 2 emissions at zero through use of an equivalent amount of verified carbon removal offsets and International Renewable Energy Certificates (IRECs).

* **Project 2: Transition to EV based food deliveries**
    Our key climate risk mitigation action is to support delivery partners in transitioning to EV bikes to undertake food deliveries. To achieve this, we generate awareness about EVs among delivery partners, collaborate with EV ecosystem players to secure access to EVs on rent and advocate for better EV products, infrastructure and financing.

    - **EV awareness:** In FY26, delivery partners were made aware of the benefits of transitioning to EVs through multiple engaging videos on the YouTube channel, EV campaigns launched within the Zomato delivery partner app and also through notifications. We also organized the second edition of EV Bazaar in Bengaluru to educate the delivery partners on the benefits of EVs and to support their transition. Since its inception, the EV Bazaar initiative has achieved significant outreach, engaging over 2,000 delivery partners and more than 20 ecosystem providers across Delhi and Bengaluru.

    - **EV access:** To facilitate easier access to EVs on rent, we have established partnerships with EV rental agencies, battery swapping companies and EV-based logistics service providers. We launched the Zomato EV rental fleet on World Environment Day 2025 in Delhi to enable rental access to quality high-speed EVs at affordable prices for delivery partners. As of March 2026, more than 1,500 EVs have been deployed across Delhi and Chennai under the Zomato EV rental fleet initiative.

    - **EV advocacy:** We continuously advocate for better EV products, infrastructure and financing for delivery partners. We are a part of global coalitions on EVs including the Climate Group’s EV100 initiative and the UNEP-supported Deliver-e Coalition. We regularly contribute to stakeholder consultations facilitated by central, state and city-level government on the topic of EVs.

    - **Delivery optimization:** Alongside supporting partners in adopting EVs for food delivery, we are continuously improving operational efficiency through delivery optimization measures that bring down delivery distances and time, thereby reducing energy consumption and its associated emissions. In FY26, delivery optimization has resulted in avoidance of 8,100 tCO2e of emissions.

    As a result of our efforts, our last-mile delivery emissions on a per km basis reduced by 11.77% in FY26 when compared to FY22. The total EV-based food deliveries increased by 50% compared to the previous year, totaling 132.3 million orders. This represented 13.5% of total food delivery orders for the same year.
