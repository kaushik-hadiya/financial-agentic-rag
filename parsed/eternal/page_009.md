## **Food delivery new initiatives**

screenshot: Zomato app interface showing Healthy mode and High Protein Breakfast Options

### **Healthy mode**

Dedicated in-app experience powered by AI, where customers can browse and order from a curated assortment of healthy dishes with nutritional information, ingredient-level insights and a healthy score for every item

screenshot: Zomato app interface showing Chef's Table

### **Chef’s table**

Curated selection of signature dishes served in tasting portions, letting customers experience the breadth of a restaurant's offering in a single order

Apart from the above, we have also been innovating and building on features such as food rescue, food on train, Zomato for Enterprise (ZFE), scheduled orders, group ordering, crowd favourites and friends’ recommendation to drive customer engagement on our platform.
