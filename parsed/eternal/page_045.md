### Pillar 3
## **Responsible business leadership**

#### **Material topics covered**

* Innovation
* Human rights
* Brand and reputation
* Health, safety and dignity for all
* Food quality, storage and transportation

For us, business leadership carries a dual mandate: (i) innovating ahead of evolving stakeholder expectations and, (ii) protecting the health, safety and dignity of everyone our businesses touch, including beyond our value chain through initiatives like Feeding India and Blinkit Ambulance service. At the same time, we also work to preserve the quality of the food across our operations while safeguarding the trust our brands have earned.
