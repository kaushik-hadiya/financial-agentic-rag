## **Additional voluntary benefits for delivery partners**

icon: insurance protection
### **Insurance protection**
Accidental, OPD, maternity cover, amongst other benefits; INR 200+ crore spent till date

icon: doctor-at-store
### **Doctor-at-store**
Facility providing free consultations and medicines to Blinkit delivery partners

icon: weekly payouts
### **Weekly payouts**
All earnings are transferred to delivery partners’ bank accounts weekly

icon: instant withdrawal
### **Instant withdrawal**
Accrued earnings can also be withdrawn at an on-demand basis

icon: instant access to tips
### **Instant access to tips**
Tips paid by customers are instantly transferred post order delivery

icon: access to in-app savings & fixed deposits
### **Access to in-app savings & fixed deposits**
Accrued savings of INR 2.5 crore in fixed and recurring deposits

icon: scholarships for children
### **Scholarships for children**
Mobilized scholarships worth INR 7+ crore for 2,250+ children of 1,700+ delivery partners

icon: period rest days
### **Period rest days**
Two paid period rest days every month for women delivery partners

*Note:*
*1. Above benefits are subject to terms.*
