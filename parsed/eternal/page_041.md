## **Re-evaluation of EV and Net Zero goals**

Our focused efforts on driving EV-based last-mile deliveries over the last few years has led to 2W EV fleet penetration reaching an estimated 2x+ of the on-road penetration of 2W EVs in India. Our learnings through this journey point to three constraints holding back mass EV adoption among delivery partners. With deliveries contributing significantly to our scope 3 emissions, EV adoption is central to our Net Zero goal:

* **High upfront costs vs. low full-time participation:** A majority of delivery partners work part-time to supplement existing incomes and prefer to use vehicles they already own or have access to. Investing in a new EV is viable only for a small share of delivery partners with long-term delivery plans
* **High need for credit but limited and expensive financing:** Most of the delivery partners interested in buying an EV require financing, but credit availability is limited. Those who can access financing often face unfavorable loan terms (higher interest rates, lower loan-to-value ratios) compared to ICE vehicles
* **Charging infrastructure gaps:** Inadequate charging and swapping infrastructure outside major cities, e.g., hometowns of delivery partners, creates range anxiety and undermines partners’ confidence in the long-term viability of owning an EV

These are ecosystem-level constraints. Solving them requires coordinated effort across multiple aggregators like us, EV manufacturers, infrastructure and financing players from both public and private sectors to facilitate a just, orderly and equitable transition away from petrol-based vehicles. We continue to contribute through awareness-building, enabling access to rental EVs and advocacy via government consultations and industry coalitions.

We are working with experts and partners to craft new sustainable transportation goals that better reflect our growth, diversification and on-ground realities. This exercise will lead to a revision of both of our 100% EV-based deliveries and Net Zero goals. We will share the outcome once it concludes.
