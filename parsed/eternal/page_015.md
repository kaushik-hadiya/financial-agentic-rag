## **E. Others**

Others segment primarily includes new initiatives that we may launch from time to time. As of Mar-26, Others segment included Bistro, Nugget, Greening India, amongst others.

screenshot: Zomato app interface showing lunch line-up

### **Bistro:**

Bistro is a quick food delivery service where it controls the entire supply chain from kitchen infrastructure to food preparation to last mile delivery, which allows it to bring down food costs materially. The intent is to focus on two demand pockets that are currently unaddressed by the existing restaurant and food delivery ecosystem - a) customers looking for high quality but low cost meals and, b) customers looking for instant snacky food.

screenshot: Nugget AI Operating System for Enterprise Outcomes

### **Nugget:**

Nugget is our AI platform for customer operations, with agents capable of not only handling conversations but also driving processes to completion across channels. Nugget powers 15+ million interactions a month across Zomato, Blinkit, District and Hyperpure, with 85%+ resolved end-to-end without human intervention. In FY26, Nugget was piloted with select external customers.

In addition to the above, Others segment also includes Zomato Payments Private Limited (ZPPL) and Zomato Local Services Private Limited (ZLSPL).
