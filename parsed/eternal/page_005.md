## **Eternal at a glance**

Eternal is more than just a name – it is a commitment to building something that deserves to last. An institution where culture outlasts strategy, where systems outlast individuals, where the work of one generation creates the foundation for the next. Today, that foundation spans four key businesses transforming how Indians dine, shop and go-out.
