## **D. B2B supplies**

image: Illustration of a Hyperpure delivery truck and a chef receiving a crate of produce

Hyperpure is our B2B supplies business offering quality food ingredients and other products to restaurants. Hyperpure operates a 1P model (i.e., owns inventory) where it sources directly from farmer producer organizations, traders and brands, and supplies to its customers through its end-to-end supply chain.

In addition, Hyperpure has also been scaling two new verticals – a) value added food supplies through its own processing facility for value added products such as sauces, spreads, pre-cut and semi-finished perishables, and b) 4PL warehousing supply chain services offering end-to-end logistics and supply chain management for restaurants including procurement, warehousing and final supply, currently serving multiple large branded chains with a pan-India presence. Customers choose Hyperpure as it acts as a single destination offering consistently high quality products with competitive pricing across categories and reliable & timely delivery.

Up until FY26, Hyperpure also had a non-restaurant business comprising B2B sales of mainly fresh food and staples to the sellers on Blinkit marketplace. This business scaled down completely during the fiscal year driven by shift to inventory ownership in quick commerce and hence, Hyperpure’s total Revenue saw a YoY decline. However, the core restaurant business continued to scale and grew 38% YoY in FY26.

Hyperpure also fulfils a core capability for each of our B2C businesses -

* **Food delivery and going-out (dining):** For restaurants, Hyperpure removes the daily stress of sourcing and inventory management. Chefs and operators get fresh, reliable ingredients every time without needing large storage space or a dedicated procurement team. This enables restaurants to run leaner operations, scale faster, and focus more on delivering a great experience to their customers. Over time, this kind of reliability builds trust, which then extends to our relationship with these restaurants in both these businesses
* **Quick commerce:** Blinkit relies on Hyperpure’s sourcing engine for fresh and perishable products. Its technology-driven, backward-integrated supply chain and warehousing infrastructure enables Blinkit to offer consistent and quality SKUs while maintaining healthy sourcing margins and minimising wastage
