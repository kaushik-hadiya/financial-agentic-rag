## **C. Going-out**

image: Illustration of people using the District app at a concert venue

Going-out is our third B2C business which addresses the ‘going-out’ needs of our customers by enabling discovery and transactions across multiple going-out experiences including dining-out, movies, sporting events, live events, concerts, outdoor activities and shopping through the District app.

FY26 was the first full year of District operating as a unified going-out platform. No other platform in India brings together this breadth of going-out use cases on a single app. As we add more use cases, engagement frequency and customer retention improve, with each category reinforcing the others.

icon: dining icon

### **Dining-out**

Discovery, table reservations and transactions across restaurants

icon: movie icon

### **Movies**

Ticketing partner for all major cinema chains pan-India

### **Events**

Ticketing for third party and co-produced events across concerts, theatre, comedy shows and others

### **Sports**

Ticketing for live sports events; exclusive ticketing partner for multiple IPL teams

### **Stores**

### **Activities**

Discovery and booking of sports facilities, theme parks, monument tours, etc. for recreational activities
