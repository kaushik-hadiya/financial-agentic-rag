## **3. Provide details of instances of engagement with and actions are taken to, address the concerns of vulnerable/ marginalized stakeholder groups.**

Eternal has identified vulnerable/ marginalized stakeholders groups both within and outside of its value chain and has taken multiple actions to address their concerns.

Below are the actions taken to address concerns of vulnerable/ marginalized groups within Eternal’s value chain:

* **Zomato’s Equitable Action for Livelihood (ZEAL) program-** Launched in FY23, our PwD delivery partner initiative has benefitted over 6,000 PwDs with a range of disabilities including locomotor and hearing and speech impairment. In FY26 alone 3,500+ PwD DPs, collectively delivered over 10 million orders and covered over 40 million kms. The program benefits include waiver of onboarding fee, assignment of short-distance deliveries only, enhanced payouts, sensitized fleet coaches and support teams to better assist delivery partners as needed. In addition, restaurant partners and end-users are notified when a PwD delivery partner is assigned to an order and requested to extend their support.

* **Zomato’s Women riding centers-** Facilitates two-wheeler riding training for women, basic digital literacy and confidence building. Through this initiative, Eternal aims to empower women by enhancing their mobility, enabling them to meet their personal needs with greater independence while also unlocking access to livelihood opportunities. Highlighted below are the benefits offered at these centers:
    - **A dedicated space** for women and their families to meet with company representatives, inquire and seek guidance on working in the gig economy.
    - **A safe space** for women to connect, support each other, and build a sense of community.
    - **Guidance to test and understand the available EV-bike options** for interested participants.

Other initiatives to ensure women safety and support:

* **24×7 SOS emergency support:** Available in 900+ cities, the emergency response system connects delivery partners to support like ambulance, police and an in-house response team.
* **Address visibility at night:** Between 7 PM and 5 AM, women delivery partners have the option to avoid locations where they may feel unsafe.
