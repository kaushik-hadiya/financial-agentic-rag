## **15. Provide details of any corrective action taken or underway to address safety-related incidents (if any) and on significant risks/ concerns arising from assessments of health & safety practices and working conditions.**

We have conducted comprehensive risk assessments and revised risk evaluations across all activities in our offices to ensure continued compliance with safety standards and best practices. Through our internal assessment we have confirmed that our operations align with the established safety protocols since there were zero safety-related incidents reported in FY26 (Principle 3, Q11), therefore no immediate corrective actions or mitigation measures were required.

We hold ISO 45001:2018 certification for our Gurugram and Bengaluru offices. For our newly leased offices we are in the process of attaining these certifications. Our Occupational Health and Safety (OH&S) management system remains aligned with the requirements of the standard, reaffirming the robustness of the framework.

In addition, an independent Human Rights assessment has been conducted for all our office locations and no major findings were reported during the financial year.
