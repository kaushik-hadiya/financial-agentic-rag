## **E. Others**

icon: 1

### **Adjusted EBITDA losses increased largely due to investments in Bistro**

Others losses increased YoY largely due to investments in Bistro, our express food delivery service where kitchen infrastructure is managed by us. We scaled to 50+ kitchens by Mar-26 across Delhi NCR and Bangalore. Early indicators have been encouraging with kitchens generating incremental demand without cannibalizing the food delivery business. We will continue to make calibrated investments towards building a scalable and profitable business here.
