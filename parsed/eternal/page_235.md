### **Impairment of Goodwill -**
For the purpose of impairment testing, goodwill acquired in a business combination is allocated to the cash generating unit (CGU) which benefits from the synergies of the acquisition. CGUs which have goodwill allocated to them are tested for impairment at least annually. The Group recognises impairment, when the carrying amount of CGU including goodwill exceeds the estimated recoverable amount of CGU. The Group’s CGUs containing goodwill are as follows:

1. India food ordering and delivery (Goodwill: INR 1,209 crore)
2. Blink Commerce Private Limited (“BCPL”)(Goodwill: INR 3,507 crore)
3. Orbgen Technologies Private Limited (“OTPL”)(Goodwill: INR 514 crore)
4. Wasteland Entertainment Private Limited (“WEPL”)(Goodwill: INR 506 crore)

The recoverable amount of India food ordering and delivery CGU is determined based on the market value of the Company. The recoverable amounts of BCPL, OTPL and WEPL CGUs are estimated based on the discounted cash flows method and comparable companies method. The significant unobservable inputs used in the estimation of recoverable amounts are revenue, discount rate, terminal growth rate and market multiple. The discount rate used for such computations is the weighted average cost of capital 13.60% to 15.60% (March 31, 2025: 11% to 26%) of the Company which contain such CGU.The estimated recoverable amounts of CGUs exceeded their carrying amounts and accordingly, no impairment was recognised. No reasonably possible change in the discount rate or terminal growth rate would cause the recoverable amount to fall shorter than the carrying amount.
