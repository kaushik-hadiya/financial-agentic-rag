Hyperpure Revenue decreased 13% YoY to INR 5,366 crore in FY26 as the non-restaurant business scaled down due to shift to inventory ownership in quick commerce. The core restaurant supplies business grew 38% YoY in FY26, driven primarily by an increase in the number of restaurant outlets served, and we expect it to keep growing at around 40% YoY over the next few years.

Profitability improved during FY26, with Adjusted EBITDA margin improving to -0.3% for the full year from -1.4% in FY25. The business achieved quarterly Adjusted EBITDA break-even for the first time in Q3FY26 and remained profitable in Q4FY26.

In the next three years, Hyperpure could achieve $1 billion in topline with 4-5% Adjusted EBITDA margin.
