Going-out NOV grew 42% YoY to INR 9,399 crore in FY26 from INR 6,618 crore in FY25, driven by continued growth across all key categories including dining-out, movies, sports and live events. Revenue grew 32% YoY to INR 973 crore in FY26.

During FY26, we added new categories such as shopping (local retail), launched District in the UAE by bringing our existing dining-out business and live event ticketing on a single app and introduced District Pass as a common loyalty program across multiple going-out use cases. Together, these initiatives strengthened the District ecosystem and supported NOV growth during the fiscal.
