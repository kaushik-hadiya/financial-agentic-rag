During FY26, we launched four new initiatives:

* **District Stores:** digitizing offline retail by enabling discovery of stores with access to deals and offers; onboarded ~5,200 outlets across 8 cities by Mar-26
* **District Play:** entered recreational sports by enabling discovery and booking of third-party sports facilities on a pay-and-play basis
* **District Pass:** launched our loyalty membership program offering benefits across movies and dining-out, to drive customer retention and incremental demand across all going-out use cases on District
* **UAE expansion:** launched District app in UAE bringing dining-out and live events onto a single app
