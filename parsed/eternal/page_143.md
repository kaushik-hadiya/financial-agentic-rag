**10. Briefly describe the waste management practices adopted in your establishments. Describe the strategy adopted by your company to reduce the usage of hazardous and toxic chemicals in your products and processes and the practices adopted to manage such wastes.**

Eternal Limited (formerly known as Zomato Limited) is a technology platform services provider. Our core business processes do not result in the generation of waste beyond office waste within which no toxic waste is generated and hazardous waste is minimal.

However, we are deeply committed to effective waste management strategies to minimize environmental impacts of waste in our offices and in our value chain.

### **Office waste management**

Our employees sit out of leased premises or co-working spaces where waste disposal is managed by the owners or main lessors of the property. In offices that are directly leased by us, we have implemented initiatives to reduce waste and promote segregation at the source.

* We promote the use of sustainable alternatives within our workplaces through provision of reusable cutlery and plates, implementation of a ‘Bring Your Own Bottle (BYOB)’ campaign, use of printing paper made from wheat straw (agri-residue). Our office coffee mugs are biodegradable and made of fallen pine needles, thereby reducing reliance on single-use items and virgin resources.
* We utilize color coded and labelled bins to ensure segregation of waste streams, supported by dedicated housekeeping teams. For e-waste, biomedical and hazardous waste, we appoint authorized vendors and obtain disposal and recycling certificates. For other waste streams such as plastic, wet waste and paper and cardboard, that are managed by landlords or facility operators, we obtain details on disposal and recycling from their appointed vendors.
* We conduct regular awareness campaigns and training initiatives on responsible consumption and disposal habits among our employees. These efforts are aimed at promoting efficient waste segregation at source.
* We continue to invest in innovative and sustainable waste management practices. For example, in FY26, we onboarded Padcare as our menstrual waste management vendor across our offices. Padcare collects used sanitary pads and recycles them into hygienic pulp and plastic, offering a safer alternative to landfilling and incineration.

In line with our environmental best practices, our Gurugram and Bengaluru offices hold ISO 14001 certification, demonstrating our commitment to effective environmental management.
