## **Adjusted EBITDA margin improved to -0.6% in FY26 with the business turning profitable from Q3FY26 onwards**

Adjusted EBITDA loss reduced to INR 277 crore in FY26 despite 100%+ YoY NOV growth with Adjusted EBITDA margin improving from -1.3% in FY25 to -0.6% in FY26. The business achieved Adjusted EBITDA break-even in Q3FY26 and reached 0.3% Adjusted EBITDA margin (% of NOV) in Q4FY26.

Improvement in profitability was driven by gross margin expansion, cost efficiencies and operating leverage. These gains were achieved despite continued investments in store expansion & customer acquisition and the heightened competitive intensity during the fiscal.

We expect the business to get to 5-6% of Adjusted EBITDA margin in steady state. Some of our more mature cities are already approaching steady state margins.
