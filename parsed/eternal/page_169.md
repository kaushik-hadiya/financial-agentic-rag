### **Familiarisation Programmes for Independent Directors**

The Company is committed to ensure that the independent directors remain well informed of material developments within the corporate and industry landscape. The directors are regularly updated on the Company’s business performance, legislative and regulatory developments, the Company’s strategy, risks and controls, ESG initiatives, and other matters considered relevant for the effective discharge of their duties.

The Board is kept apprised of all significant developments occurring between scheduled meetings enabling directors to provide strategic guidance and maintain continuous oversight of the Company’s operations.
