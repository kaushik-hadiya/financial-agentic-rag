## 3 **Cash balance decreased by INR 852 crore in FY26**

Consolidated cash balance decreased to INR 17,972 crore as of Mar-26 despite increase in Adjusted EBITDA and treasury income, primarily due to investments in capex and net working capital in the quick commerce business. Capex increased on account of rapid expansion of Blinkit’s store and warehouse network, while net working capital increased following the transition to inventory ownership. We expect these investments in quick commerce to generate ROCE of at least 40% in steady state.
