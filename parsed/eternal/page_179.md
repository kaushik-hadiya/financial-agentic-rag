## **6. Means of communication**

The Company maintains transparent communication with its shareholders through dedicated channels, including annual general meetings, periodic letters, newspaper publications, and investor conferences. These initiatives are designed to enhance dialogue, strengthen relationships, and promote long-term sustainable value for all stakeholders.

* The quarterly financial results, shareholders’ letters, annual and event-based stock exchange filings are publicly accessible on the websites of NSE at <u>www.nseindia.com</u>, BSE at <u>www.bseindia.com</u> and website of the Company at <u>www.eternal.com</u>.
* The information regarding the availability of financial results, together with QR code and web link for accessing such financial results, is generally published in Financial Express (English newspaper) circulating substantially in the whole of India and in Jansatta (vernacular (Hindi) newspaper) in Delhi.
* The Company provides quarterly updates to the shareholders/ investors on the business performance and financial results through the shareholders’ letters. The Company also hosts earnings conference calls every quarter to address investor queries on the Company’s performance and financial results.
