## <u>**Report</u> on Corporate Governance (CGR)**

### **1. Company’s philosophy on Corporate Governance**

Our corporate governance philosophy is rooted in integrity, transparency, accountability, and responsible business conduct, ensuring the protection of stakeholder interests and driving long-term value creation. This commitment to ethical and sustainable practices shapes our organizational decision-making and drives compliance with applicable laws in line with the charter documents of the Company in both letter and spirit. At the core of our governance framework is the strategic oversight and independent judgement provided by our Board, who guide the Company’s affairs and supervise the leadership team’s day-to-day operations through specialized committees. To further foster a robust culture of accountability, we have implemented a comprehensive policy framework that includes specific codes of conduct for employees, senior management, and the Board, alongside strict guidelines for business partners and for prevention of insider trading.

### **2. Board of Directors**

The Board steers the strategy, performance, and governance, establishing a robust operational framework to drive sustainable, long-term value for all stakeholders. The Board maintains independent oversight to ensure management consistently delivers on its commitments. The directors bring together a diverse mix of specialized skills and deep industry experience with strong leadership to execute its responsibilities with high efficiency.

The Board has 7 (Seven) committees comprising of Board members to discharge the responsibilities assigned to them.

### **Composition and inter-se relationship**

During the financial year under review, the composition of the Board was in conformity with Regulation 17 of the SEBI Listing Regulations and Section 149 and 152 of the Act. The Company operates with a one-tier system featuring an optimum mix of non-executive directors including women directors.

The Board comprises 6 (six) directors of whom 4 (four) are independent directors (including 3 (three) women independent directors) and 2 (two) non-executive directors, (including 1 (one) nominee director). None of the directors share any inter-se relationships, each one of them is independent of the other, and all non-executive directors are under the age of seventy-five years.

### **Board Meetings**

Our Board meets at regular intervals to deliberate and decide on operations, key strategic issues, and statutory matters. To maintain consistent momentum, the gap between two consecutive meetings never exceeds 120 days. The notice, detailed agenda together with relevant notes and other material information are generally shared with the directors sufficiently in advance of Board meetings, enabling effective deliberations and informed decision-making.
