#### **c. A brief on types of customers**\*

For the purpose of this BRSR disclosure, we have three types of customers as defined below:

1. **End-users of our platforms-** End-users are customers who use our platforms to search and discover restaurants, read and share reviews, view and upload photos, order food, book a table, discover and transact across going out experiences.
2. **Restaurant partners-** We provide restaurant partners with industry-specific technological tools which enable them to engage and acquire customers to grow their business while also providing a reliable and efficient last-mile delivery service.
3. **Business partners-** These include other partners onboarded on our platform to engage and acquire customers. We also provide business partners technological tools that enable customer discovery, participation in going-out experiences by offering curated offers and discounts and to manage certain parts of their business operations.
