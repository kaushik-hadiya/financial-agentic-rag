**2. a. Does the entity have procedures in place for sustainable sourcing? (Yes/No)**
Yes.

Eternal Limited continued to strengthen its Sustainable Supply Chain program in FY26 to promote responsible sourcing practices across its value chain. Initiatives taken in FY26 panned across these three areas:

* **Supplier assessment:** Our supplier assessment on ESG parameters is integrated into our supplier onboarding process. We regularly evaluate our upstream suppliers through an ESG questionnaire (aligned with the requirements of BRSR Core KPIs) enabling consistent monitoring and improvement across our supply chain.
* **Capacity building and engagement:**
    - Conducted workshops to communicate sustainability expectations to suppliers, with focused discussions on health & safety and human rights practices in the supply chain attended by 200+ suppliers.
    - Held one-on-one interactions with key supplier categories to discuss ESG practices and improvement areas.
    - Circulated human rights learning modules for suppliers to strengthen awareness and responsible business practices.
    - Conducted road safety and health & safety training for delivery partners as part of the onboarding process.
* **Supplier partnership and recognition:**
    - Initiated efforts to recognise suppliers demonstrating strong ESG performance, responsible business practices and contributing to positive outcomes.
    - Continued supplier engagement through <u>esg-suppliers@eternal.com</u>, to facilitate onboarding of sustainable suppliers and addressing supplier-related ESG queries.
    - Expanded sourcing from within India by working with 1800+ MSMEs in FY25.
