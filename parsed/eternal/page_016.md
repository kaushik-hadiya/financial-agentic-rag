NOV (B2C business) grew 55% YoY to INR 95,959 crore in FY26 driven by growth across all three B2C businesses:

* Food delivery NOV grew 16% YoY to INR 37,993 crore
* Quick commerce NOV grew 117% YoY to INR 48,567 crore
* Going-out NOV grew 42% YoY to INR 9,399 crore

FY26 was the first year when our NOV (B2C business) crossed $10 billion - a milestone that took us 18 years to reach. We expect annual NOV to double to $20 billion in the next two years.

B2B business Hyperpure’s Revenue declined 13% YoY to INR 5,366 crore as the non-restaurant business scaled down following the shift to inventory ownership in quick commerce.

Consolidated Adjusted Revenue grew 159% YoY to INR 55,903 crore in FY26. The sharp increase was primarily driven by the shift to inventory ownership in quick commerce where Revenue now includes the full monetary value of goods sold (and not just the marketplace commission). On a like-for-like basis (excluding the impact of inventory ownership shift), Adjusted Revenue grew 61% YoY.

*Notes:*
*1. Like-for-like (“LFL”) consolidated Adjusted Revenue is calculated as consolidated Adjusted Revenue (-) Revenue from Hyperpure’s non-restaurant business (-) cost of goods sold in case of own inventory sales in quick commerce.*
*2. FY25 Adjusted Revenue shown above consolidates the acquired entertainment ticketing business from 27-Aug-24 onwards (transaction closing date).*
