## **Our updated materiality matrix**

We conducted our first comprehensive materiality assessment in FY22 to identify our key sustainability themes. The eight themes identified were reflective of the company we were at the time. Food delivery was our single large business, complemented by a dining-out offering and a much smaller Hyperpure business.

Since then, the organization has grown multi-fold. Quick commerce, which did not exist as a business for us in FY22, is now our largest business by NOV. Dining-out has grown into a full-fledged going-out offering spanning dining, events, movies, shopping and other use cases. Hyperpure has scaled meaningfully and now supports each of our B2C businesses. With this evolution, our stakeholder universe has also broadened and so have the ESG themes that are relevant to us.

To align our ESG priorities with the business we are today, we refreshed our materiality assessment in FY26 through a double materiality assessment covering all four key businesses. A double materiality lens evaluates each topic on two dimensions:

* Impact our businesses have on people and the environment
* Financial implications of sustainability-related issues on our businesses

Nine material topics were identified through this assessment.
