<tr><td>3. <strong>Voluntary recycling</strong></td><td>• In FY23, Zomato app launched the 100% Plastic Neutral Deliveries initiative. While the choice of packaging used for food deliveries rests with our restaurant partners, we are committed to mitigating the impact of this packaging on the environment through voluntary recycling. To estimate the volume of plastic used by restaurants for packaging, we engaged an expert third party to review data from orders by dish type, restaurant location and price of meals. This data was supplemented by an independent survey of the packaging practices of over 600 restaurants. Zomato then engaged authorized waste management agencies to undertake recycling of plastic waste equal to the volume estimated by the third party study of restaurant packaging.<br /><br /><u>Refer to this link for further details</u></td><td>• Since launch, Eternal has voluntarily recycled 60,000 MT of plastic waste from FY23- FY26 (15,000 MT was recycled in FY26 alone) fully meeting the target of 100% plastic neutral deliveries as per independent estimates.</td></tr>

Customers can also discover these restaurants faster by typing ‘low plastic packaging’ in the search bar or by using the ‘low plastic packaging’ filter option.<br /><br />Our aim is to motivate more restaurants to make the switch from plastic to sustainable packaging alternatives while making it easier for environment-conscious and/or packaging-conscious customers to discover and order from these restaurants easily.
