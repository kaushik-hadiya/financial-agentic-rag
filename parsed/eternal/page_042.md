## Pillar 2
### **Inclusive growth**

#### **Material topics covered**
* Stakeholder value creation
* Human capital development

Reaching customers at scale has, traditionally, required investments most small businesses cannot afford. Our platforms lower these barriers, giving small businesses and upcoming entrepreneurs access to millions of customers and enabling them to compete with larger players on a level playing field. In this process, we also help generate income opportunities for lakhs of riders and merchant partners.

We intend to deepen this over time by (i) helping more small businesses find growth on our platforms, (ii) expanding earning opportunities for delivery partners and (iii) investing in the development of our own people, whose capabilities ultimately determine how well we serve everyone else. Our work under this pillar spans four themes. The table below summarizes our key initiatives and progress under each theme in FY26.
