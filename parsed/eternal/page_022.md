Quick commerce NOV grew 117% YoY to INR 48,567 crore in FY26, surpassing food delivery NOV scale for the full year. NOV growth was driven by 116% YoY growth in Orders while NAOV remained broadly flat (0.3% YoY growth). Order volume growth was driven by 117% YoY increase in average monthly transacting customers, on the back of continued investments in geographic expansion and customer acquisition.

Blinkit has scaled at a NOV CAGR of 104% since FY23 (year of Blinkit acquisition). The long-term opportunity remains significant, and we expect NOV to grow at 60%+ CAGR over the next three years. This growth will be driven by assortment expansion, geographic coverage and demand densification while improving the quality and reliability of our service.
