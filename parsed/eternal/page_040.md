## **Climate action and circular economy**

#### **Material topics covered**
* Climate action and circular economy

We recognize the impact our operations have on the environment. We also believe that businesses built to last cannot treat climate change and its consequences as distant concerns. They are operating realities that will only grow in significance.

We are committed to minimizing our environmental footprint while preparing for climate change, ensuring long-term business growth and resilience. Our work under this pillar spans three themes. The table below summarizes our key initiatives and progress under each theme in FY26.
