## **ANNEXURE – II**

### **REPORT ON CSR ACTIVITIES**

#### **Brief outline on CSR policy of the Company**

The Company’s CSR policy reflects its commitment to creating sustainable value for society while conducting its business in a responsible, ethical and environmentally conscious manner. The policy is founded on the belief that businesses have an important role to play in contributing towards inclusive and sustainable development by creating a positive impact on the communities and the environment around their areas of operation.

The Company voluntarily undertakes initiatives with the objective of supporting programmes that create meaningful, measurable and long-term social impact. It further enables the Company to undertake projects across the areas including initiatives relating to healthcare, education, livelihood enhancement, environmental sustainability, hunger eradication, disaster relief and other activities that address evolving societal needs. The Company believes that its CSR initiatives will be implemented in a transparent, accountable and efficient manner, with appropriate oversight and periodic review to ensure effective utilisation of resources and achievement of the intended outcomes.

The CSR policy also sets out the roles and responsibilities of the Board and the CSR Committee in providing strategic direction and oversight, including the approval, monitoring and review of CSR projects and expenditure, if any. During the financial year under review, there was no change in the CSR policy of the Company. The CSR policy is available on the Company’s website.
