## **A. Food delivery**

image: illustration of a Zomato delivery partner handing a bag to a customer at a train station

Zomato operates a B2C technology platform that provides customers with a seamless, on-demand solution to discover local restaurants, order food, and have it delivered reliably and quickly.

Every order on Zomato requires real-time coordination. A meal has to be prepared by a third-party restaurant, picked up by an independent delivery partner navigating unpredictable traffic, and delivered while the food is still hot — executed at a scale of millions of orders per day with no room for error. Over time, we have invested in building systems that make this possible, reliably and at scale across the length and breadth of the country. As of Mar-26, our food delivery operations were present across 900+ cities pan-India.
