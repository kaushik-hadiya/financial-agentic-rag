### **2. STATE OF COMPANY’S AFFAIRS / OVERVIEW:**

Financial year 2025-26 was a year of structural reset for the Company. Against a backdrop of slower electric two-wheeler penetration, heightened competitive intensity and service-related execution challenges, the Company recalibrated its operating model from rapid expansion towards sustainable growth, stronger unit economics and greater cash discipline.

During the year, the Company focused on strengthening service operations, improving product quality, rationalising its retail footprint, reducing operating costs and progressing Cell manufacturing from validation to commercial deployment. While these actions were undertaken against lower volumes, they established a leaner and more scalable foundation for future growth.

Despite the operating challenges, the Company achieved several important milestones:

* **Margin Expansion and Improved Cash Flow:** Consolidated gross margin increased from 17.9% in FY25 to 30.6% in FY26, with Q4 FY26 gross margin reaching 38.5%. Consolidated cash flow from operations improved from negative ₹2,391 crore in FY25 to negative ₹775 crore in FY26, while Q4 FY26 became the Company’s first positive operating cash-flow quarter.

* **Operating Model Reset:** Consolidated operating expenses, reduced from ₹779 crore in Q4 FY25 to ₹383 crore in Q4 FY26. This improvement was driven by network rationalisation, higher workforce productivity, automation, tighter operating governance and a leaner corporate cost structure.
