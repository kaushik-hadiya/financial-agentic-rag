## **One Cell platform. Multiple opportunities**

The Gigafactory provides a common technology and manufacturing base for products spanning electric mobility, household storage, distributed commercial infrastructure and large-format energy storage. Across these applications, the platform brings together Cell technology, battery-management systems, power electronics, thermal management, software and system integration.

### **Mobility**

Ola's mobility portfolio spans electric scooters and motorcycles across value and performance segments. Progressive integration of in-house NMC and LFP Cells is intended to strengthen control over battery performance, range, cost and supply, while allowing the same core vehicle architecture to serve different customer requirements.

### **Shakti: homes and distributed infrastructure**

The Shakti portfolio extends the Cell platform into distributed energy storage. At the household level, it addresses power backup for houses and storage alongside rooftop-solar connections. Larger Shakti formats extend the portfolio to telecom towers, petrol pumps, quick-commerce dark stores and other distributed retail and commercial infrastructure, where reliable backup power and lower dependence on conventional systems are important operating requirements.

### **Mahashakti: commercial, industrial and utility storage**

Mahashakti extends the portfolio into larger systems for data centres, factories and independent power producers, as well as renewable-energy integration and other commercial, industrial and utility-scale applications. Together, Shakti and Mahashakti take the same technology platform from individual homes to distributed infrastructure and large energy users.

### **External Cell applications**

Beyond Ola's own mobility and storage products, the Company is developing external Cell-sales opportunities across global automotive and industrial OEMs, defence, UAVs and other strategic applications. For defence and UAV applications, energy density, reliability, safety and domestic supply security are critical. As the only commercial lithium-ion Cell producer based in India, Ola can support a more locally anchored supply chain for these national-security-linked applications.
