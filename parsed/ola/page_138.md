5. **Has the entity implemented a mechanism for Zero Liquid Discharge? If yes, provide details of its coverage and implementation.**

* Multiple Effect Evaporator (MEE)
* Agitated Thin Film Dryer (ATFD).

The Company follows a comprehensive water stewardship strategy based on the principles of reduce, reuse, recycle, and recharge, reinforcing its commitment to sustainable and responsible water management.
