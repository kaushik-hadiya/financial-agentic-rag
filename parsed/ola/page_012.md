### **Financial Pattern: Lower Revenue, Stronger Economics**

Despite a 50.1% contraction in revenue, FY26 consolidated gross margin expanded to 30.6% from 17.9% in FY25, demonstrating a material improvement in product economics. Cash flow from operations improved from an outflow of ₹2,391 crore in FY25 to an outflow of ₹775 crore in FY26, while free cash flow improved from an outflow of ₹3,367 crore to an outflow of ₹1,492 crore. The Company therefore exited the year at a lower revenue base, but with stronger margins, lower cash burn and a more efficient operating structure.

The Gen 3 platform also reduced recurring service requirements. Warranty cost for Gen 3 was approximately 70% lower than Gen 2, while total warranty cost declined from ₹555 crore in FY25 to ₹59 crore in FY26. These improvements demonstrate the impact of product maturity and service redesign on both customer outcomes and unit economics.
