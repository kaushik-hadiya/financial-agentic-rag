## **Building prosperity ecosystems**

### Community-owned solutions for health, livelihoods and culture

Ola Foundation works to strengthen agency, well-being and opportunity within communities. Its approach begins by understanding local aspirations and barriers, and then co-creating interventions with women, frontline workers, public systems and community institutions. The objective is to develop community-driven operating models that are locally relevant, capable of being replicated and sustainable beyond individual programme cycles.

The Foundation's work is organised across three interconnected thematic areas. Health and wellness interventions focus on preventive care, maternal and adolescent health, nutrition, anaemia and non-communicable diseases. Livelihood programmes equip women with skills, entrepreneurial capabilities and market linkages. Culture and heritage initiatives seek to preserve knowledge, strengthen identity and encourage women and young people to carry India's cultural traditions forward. Technology and partnerships act as common enablers across these areas.
