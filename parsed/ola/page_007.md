## **FutureFactory**

Built for Scale. Engineered for Advantage

**Futurefactory is where Ola Electric’s technology, manufacturing and product platforms come together - turning engineering into products at scale**

Located at the heart of Ola Electric’s manufacturing ecosystem, the Futurefactory was built with a clear objective: create a manufacturing platform capable of moving with the speed of technology and the scale of India.

From groundbreaking to first production in just eight months, the facility represents a manufacturing model designed around speed, integration and scalability. Today, it has an installed production capacity of 1 million electric two-wheelers annually, with a modular architecture designed to support significantly higher scale.

**But the Futurefactory is more than capacity.**
Its value lies in how product engineering, technology ownership and manufacturing come together within one integrated system. This allows new product architectures and engineering improvements to move rapidly from design to industrialisation - while common platforms and vertically integrated technologies help create a structurally efficient cost base.

**Manufacturing at the speed of innovation.**
As our portfolio expands across scooters, motorcycles and future mobility formats, the Futurefactory gives us the flexibility to adapt manufacturing around changing products, segments and customer needs. Instead of creating an entirely new manufacturing system for every product, common architectures and modular platforms allow us to reuse engineering, manufacturing processes and infrastructure across multiple products. That makes the factory not only a production asset, but a platform for the next generation of Ola products.
