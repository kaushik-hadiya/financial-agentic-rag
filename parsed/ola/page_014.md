## **Six Fundamentals Strengthened in FY26**

FY26 can be understood through six operating fundamentals. Each one moved through the year, and each one reinforces the others. Together they explain why the company can emerge from a lower-volume year with a stronger operating platform.

icon: service

### **Service**

Faster turnaround, lower backlog, better parts availability and tighter daily governance improved the ownership experience and restored operating reliability.

icon: product quality

### **Product Quality**

Gen 3 maturity, diagnostics and a repair-led service model reduced failure and warranty intensity.

icon: gross margin

### **Gross Margin**

Vertical integration, product architecture, pricing and cost reduction drove consolidated gross margin to 30.6% for FY26 and 38.5% in Q4

icon: cost base

### **Cost Base**

Network rationalisation, lower fixed overheads, productivity initiatives and tighter governance established a leaner operating platform.

icon: cash discipline

### **Cash Discipline**

Working-capital release, lower operating expenditure and stronger product economics materially reduced operating cash outflow and free-cash-flow burn.

icon: cell scale

### **Cell Scale**

The 4680 Bharat Cell entered commercial deployment, 2.5 GWh became operational and the installation required for 6 GWh was largely completed.

The important point is not that every problem was solved. It is that the business spent the year turning each issue into a system: service into measurable operating routines, quality into platform engineering, cost into structural redesign, and cells into a commercial technology roadmap.
