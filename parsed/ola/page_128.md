## **10. Health and Safety management system:**

**a. Whether an occupational health and safety management system has been implemented by the entity? (Yes/ No).If yes, the coverage such system?**

Yes. Occupational Health and Safety Management System is in place.

1. Dissemination of Occupational Health & Safety policy
2. EHS Guidelines on Hazard Identification & Risk Assessment is in place to assess the Risk and put controls in place to reduce the Risk as low as reasonably practicable
3. EHS Guidelines in place for incident reporting & investigation
4. EHS Guidelines on Factory Layout
5. SOP on Work Permits System

**b. What are the processes used to identify work-related hazards and assess risks on a routine and non-routine basis by the entity?**

Our workplaces are equipped with Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment (HIRA) systems, We regularly conduct health and safety audits and carry out periodic occupational health surveillance to ensure a safe working environment.

Additionally, the workplace hazards are identified through routine and non-routine activities.

1. HIRA conducted for all the routine activities.
2. The Work Permit System and Job Safety Analysis is in place to mitigate the hazards and Risk for Nonroutine activities.
3. Work instructions /SOP have been prepared for all the routine activities
4. Schedule has been prepared for workplace inspection; Fire & Safety Audit is in place to assess the Non-conformities and initiate action accordingly.
5. Safety trainings are conducted on different topics.
6. Health campaigns are conducted.

**c. Whether you have processes for workers to report the work-related hazards and to remove themselves from such risks. (Yes/No)**

Yes, employees are encouraged to report any unsafe or unhealthy working conditions to their immediate supervisor or directly to the safety department. The safety team will promptly investigate the situation, take appropriate corrective actions, and review the Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment (HIRA). An incident report will be generated, and a safety representative will be dispatched to the location to conduct an investigation.

Additionally, OEM has the template for all the ground workers to report all the work-related hazards & risk without any barriers:

1. Incident reporting template has been created and displayed across the location to report all the identified hazards & risks.
2. The Safety committee meeting has been formed across all level of employees and taking feedback about work place hazards.

**d. Do the employees/ worker of the entity have access to non-occupational medical and healthcare services?**

Yes, all employees, including contractor workers, have round-the-clock access to the clinics located in our factories for non-work-related medical services.

Additionally:

1. OEM has a fully pledged Occupational Health Centre operating 24/7 inside the premises with all the facilities to provide first aid to any medical emergency (including both occupational or any personal illness)
2. OHC is accessible for all employees, including contract workers, for receiving first aid to any Occupational related Injury and treatment for Personal Illness.
