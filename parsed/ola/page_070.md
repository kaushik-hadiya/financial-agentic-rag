## **Subsequent Developments and Outlook**

### Post-Year-End Operating Update

Q1 FY27 provided initial evidence that higher activity could be supported by the reset operating structure. Orders increased by 96% quarter on quarter to 44,071 units, deliveries increased by 94% to 38,443 units and registrations increased by 97% to 43,921 units. Market share increased from 5.1% in Q4 FY26 to 8.4% in Q1 FY27.

Automotive revenue increased by 72% quarter on quarter to ₹455 crore, Automotive gross margin stood at 30.3%, and consolidated operating expenditure reduced by 21% quarter on quarter to ₹303 crore from ₹383 crore. The Company also completed a ₹780 crore qualified institutional placement during Q1 FY27, strengthening liquidity for the next phase of execution. These indicators relate to the period after FY26 and should not be interpreted as establishing a sustained recovery.

### Post-Year-End Product Portfolio Developments

#### Mobility portfolio

After the end of FY26, the Company announced the S1Z, its most accessible scooter range, with bookings commencing at a starting price of ₹79,999. The S1Z is the Company’s first scooter range powered by its domestically developed Bharat Cell LFP technology and has been designed to address the value-oriented segment of the electric two-wheeler market.

The product represents the next stage of the Company’s Cell-to-vehicle strategy by combining internally manufactured LFP Cells with the Automotive product platform. The S1Z is also expected to be among the first products scaled through the Company’s dealer-operated distribution channel. Product performance, production ramp-up and deliveries will remain subject to manufacturing and market execution.

#### Energy-products portfolio

Subsequent to year-end, the Company further developed the market-facing roadmap for its energy-storage portfolio. Shakti is intended to address distributed residential and commercial storage requirements, with applications across homes, solar-linked installations, telecom towers, petrol pumps, quick-commerce dark stores and smaller commercial and industrial facilities.

Mahashakti is being developed for larger commercial, industrial and utility-scale storage requirements. Potential applications and customer segments include factories, data centres, independent power producers, renewable-energy developers, utilities and grid-connected storage projects. Together, these products broaden the application of the Company’s Cell technology beyond captive Automotive consumption and create additional potential demand for the 6 GWh manufacturing platform.

### FY27 Priorities and Outlook: Converting the Reset into Sustainable Growth

In Automotive, the principal priorities for FY27 are to maintain service consistency, rebuild productive volumes, develop dealer-led distribution, strengthen the product portfolio and deepen engagement with the installed customer base. Growth will be evaluated through gross margin, contribution, cash conversion and customer outcomes rather than market share or volumes in isolation.

In Cells, the priority is to commission and utilise the 6 GWh platform, improve manufacturing yield and quality, increase own-Cell integration and convert the most mature external opportunities into qualified commercial sales. Demand development across captive mobility, energy storage, global OEMs and specialised applications will be aligned with manufacturing readiness, working capital and commercial economics.

Across both businesses, the Company will continue to use vertical integration, software and artificial intelligence to improve operating execution while maintaining safety, governance and capital discipline. The reset cost base and stronger gross-margin profile create the potential for operating leverage as demand and asset utilisation improve. The timing and extent of this improvement will remain dependent on demand, pricing, product mix, commodity costs, service consistency, manufacturing performance, incentive eligibility and access to capital.
