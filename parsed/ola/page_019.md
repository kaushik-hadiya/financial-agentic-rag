## **Taking healthcare to the last mile**

### Moto Aarogyam and the SAHELI decision-support layer

In remote and tribal parts of Krishnagiri, access to preventive healthcare is constrained by distance, difficult terrain, limited transport and the indirect cost of travelling to fixed facilities. Moto Aarogyam addresses this gap by taking healthcare directly to communities. Trained field associates travel on electric scooters equipped with portable screening kits and work alongside frontline health workers to provide blood-pressure and blood-glucose screening, anaemia testing, maternal-health monitoring, counselling, referrals and follow-up support.

Between 27 January 2025 and 31 March 2026, Moto Aarogyam reached 30,741 people across six blocks of Krishnagiri district. The tribal blocks of Thally and Kelamangalam accounted for 46.9% of programme reach, while women represented 75.9% of the people served. One in four respondents said the programme provided their first screening opportunity, and 99% requested that the service continue.

SAHELI ActiQuity AI adds a technology-enabled decision-support layer to this physical reach. Designed for frontline health workers, the platform provides protocol-based, step-by-step guidance for conditions including anaemia, hypertension, gestational diabetes and maternal complications. Its offline functionality, Tamil-enabled speech-to-text interface and bilingual outputs are designed around the realities of field deployment. Together, Moto Aarogyam and SAHELI combine mobility, point-of-care technology, community trust and decision support into an integrated last-mile healthcare model.
