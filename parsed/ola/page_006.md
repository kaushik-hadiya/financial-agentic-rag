## **One Year. Four Stages. One Stronger Platform.**

FY25 established the technology, products and manufacturing infrastructure required to participate in India's electric transition. FY26 focused on strengthening the operating model behind that platform - improving service, product economics, cost discipline, cash generation and Cell-manufacturing readiness.

### **Q1 FY26 - PIVOT**

The Company shifted from growth at any cost to balanced and profitable growth. Gen 3 scaled, consolidated gross margin reached 25.8%, and actions began to reduce operating expenditure and cash burn.

photo: Ola electric motorcycle

### **Q2 FY26 - PROOF**

Consolidated gross margin increased to 30.9%. The Automotive business demonstrated improving operating economics, while HyperService and the 2.5 GWh Cell platform moved into execution.

photo: Ola 4680 battery cells

### **Q3 FY26 - RESET**

Service recovery became the foremost operating priority. Retail and cost structures were redesigned, the 4680 Bharat Cell entered commercial deployment, and Ola Shakti broadened the addressable market beyond Mobility.

photo: Ola service technician

### **Q4 FY26 - TRACTION**

Consolidated gross margin reached 38.5%, the quarter generated positive consolidated cash flow from operations, service metrics improved materially, and installation required for the 6 GWh Cell platform was largely completed.
