## Building a diversified demand pipeline

The Cell business is being developed across multiple demand pools. Captive Automotive consumption provides foundational demand and enables close integration between Cell and vehicle engineering. Energy-storage and external applications provide additional routes to commercial utilisation, while discussions with global OEMs are intended to develop a pipeline for external Cell sales.

The Company is also evaluating applications across defence, unmanned aerial vehicles and related strategic sectors. Domestic Cell manufacturing can provide greater control over technology, traceability and supply-chain continuity for applications where national security and localisation are important.

The identified pipeline includes more than 35 GWh of opportunities through signed memoranda of understanding. These opportunities vary in maturity and are not equivalent to firm orders, committed revenue or contracted cash flow.
