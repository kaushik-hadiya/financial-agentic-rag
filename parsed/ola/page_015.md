## **Gen 3 Engineering Maturity Becomes Business Quality**

Gen 3 represents more than a product upgrade. Its common architecture, shared components and integrated software reduce complexity across manufacturing, diagnostics, service and spare parts. Lower failure rates and more efficient repairs reduce warranty and service costs, supporting stronger gross margins as volumes recover.

MoveOS and MoveOS+ extend this platform through over-the-air updates, connected diagnostics and digital features. Together, hardware and software create a more consistent ownership experience and provide a base for lifetime customer engagement.

### **A common platform creates compounding advantages.**

Shared architecture, common components, software calibration and increasingly common cell integration reduce duplication across price points. This allows different products to serve different customer needs while retaining the same underlying technology logic.

### **Quality improved with every generation.**

The company reported sharply lower warranty behaviour on Gen 3 relative to earlier generations. This matters because quality compounds: fewer failures reduce service load; lower service load improves customer experience; lower warranty cost strengthens margins; stronger margins create more headroom for product investment.

### **Roadster expanded the addressable market.**

The Roadster family extended Ola beyond scooters and into India's much larger motorcycle category. The motorcycle platform brought new requirements - performance, range, thermal stability and highway capability - while still benefiting from shared engineering, software and cell platforms.

### **Software keeps the product moving after delivery.**

MoveOS continued to differentiate the ownership experience, while software monetisation through MoveOS+ showed that the digital layer could contribute not only to features and diagnostics but also to lifetime value.
