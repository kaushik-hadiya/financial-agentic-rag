## **PRINCIPLE 9- BUSINESSES SHOULD ENGAGE WITH AND PROVIDE VALUE TO THEIR CONSUMERS IN A RESPONSIBLE MANNER**

### **Essential Indicators**

1. Describe the mechanisms in place to receive and respond to consumer complaints and feedback.

   We offer customers several channels to raise complaints and seek assistance.

   i. Customer Application (C-App)

   - Customers can use the dedicated C-App to log support requests and report issues.
   - The application enables efficient tracking and resolution of customer concerns.
   - Information submitted through the app, such as order details, issue descriptions, location, and nearest service center, is captured and managed through Salesforce, our customer relationship management (CRM) platform.

   ii. Alternative Support Channels In addition to the C-App, customers may contact us through other support channels, including:

   - **Customer Service Helpline:** For direct assistance and issue resolution over the phone.
   - **Email Support:** A publicly available email address for customer queries, feedback, and complaints.

   iii. Benefits

   - **Multiple Points of Contact:** Customers can choose from various communication channels based on their preference and convenience.
   - **Insight-Driven Service Improvement:** Customer data and feedback captured through Salesforce are analyzed to identify trends and improve service quality.
   - **Enhanced Accessibility:** The availability of phone and email support ensures that assistance remains accessible to customers with different communication preferences.
