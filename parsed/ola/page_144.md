## **PRINCIPLE 7- BUSINEESS, WHEN ENGAGING IN INFLUENCING PUBLIC AND REGULATORY POLICY, SHOULD DO SO IN A MANNER THAT IS RESPONSIBLE AND TRANSPARENT**

Engaging in public and regulatory policy should be done responsibly and transparently. By advocating for policies that benefit society, businesses can contribute positively to the regulatory landscape and build trust with the public and policymakers. We understand the power of public policy to shape the world around us, and we are committed to advocating for policies that create a positive societal impact.
