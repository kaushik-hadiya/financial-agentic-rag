### **Essential Indicators**

**1. **Describe the processes for identifying key stakeholder groups of the entity**.**

The Company is committed to maintaining continuous and meaningful engagement with its stakeholders, recognizing their importance in creating long-term value and building strong partnerships. To strengthen this approach, the Company seeks to institutionalize stakeholder engagement through a structured and formalized process. This process facilitates regular interaction with stakeholder groups to understand their expectations, identify potential risks and concerns, address key challenges, and gain insights into future opportunities and priorities, enabling informed and sustainable decision-making
