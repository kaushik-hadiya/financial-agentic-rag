## **GigaFactory**

Researching, Engineering and Manufacturing in India

**From cell technology to energy infrastructure, the Ola Gigafactory is building a platform designed to power mobility today and a broader energy ecosystem tomorrow**

The Ola Gigafactory represents a strategic step in deepening our vertical integration - taking battery technology from research and product development into commercial-scale manufacturing.

Built in less than **18 months from first pillar to first cell**, the facility has reached 6 GWh of installed capacity, supported by over **₹3,000 crore of gross block**. Its modular architecture has been designed for further expansion, while supporting multiple cell chemistries and form factors on a common manufacturing platform. But scale alone is not the differentiator.

The deeper advantage lies in the technology behind the factory. Ola owns the product and process IP in-house, backed by a **400+ member team** and more than **350 patents filed**. This allows cell design, manufacturing processes and end-product integration to evolve together - strengthening control over performance, cost and localisation.

### **From validation to industrial scale**

FY26 marked an important transition for the cell business - from developing and validating the Bharat Cell to integrating in-house cells into mobility products and building the foundation for new energy applications.

Our cell platform is being engineered beyond a single chemistry or form factor. The roadmap extends across **NMC, LFP, LMFP, sodium and solid-state technologies**, while leveraging a common 46-series architecture. At the heart of this manufacturing platform is our dry-electrode process, designed to reduce manufacturing intensity compared with conventional wet-electrode processes. The benefits including lower capex, lower factory footprint and significantly lower energy usage.
