## **Technology and Operating Enablers**

### Software and AI

Software, artificial intelligence and operating data are being applied across customer acquisition, fulfilment, service, warranty assessment, manufacturing and Cell research. In Automotive, these capabilities support lead engagement, service diagnostics and operating visibility. In Cell R&D and manufacturing, they support technical analysis, performance modelling, root-cause investigation, yield improvement and quality management.

Technology adoption will be evaluated through measurable outcomes including conversion, cycle time, first-time resolution, error reduction, yield, quality and cost. Material decisions affecting safety, customer redress, warranty, legal rights or employment will remain subject to appropriate human oversight, access controls, validation, cybersecurity and data-governance requirements.

### Manufacturing and supply-chain resilience

Greater control over motors, electronics, software, battery systems and Cells can improve coordination across product development and reduce exposure to supplier-development cycles. It also increases the Company’s responsibility for validation, traceability and production quality. Internal technologies will therefore be scaled after meeting defined performance, safety, reliability, yield and cost requirements.

Supply-chain localisation will be evaluated alongside quality, availability and cost. Common data across procurement, manufacturing, quality and inventory will support earlier identification of shortages, lead-time changes and quality trends.

## **Human Capital and Organisational Effectiveness**

The FY26 operating reset included changes to workforce deployment, reporting structures and accountability. The Company simplified roles, reduced duplication and brought decision-making closer to operating issues. As activity increases, management will continue to monitor productivity, workload, capability gaps and critical-role continuity while prioritising talent investment in engineering, Cell R&D, manufacturing, quality, service, sales and dealer support.
