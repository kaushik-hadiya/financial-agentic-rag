**c. A brief on types of customers**

Ola Electric serves a broad customer base through its electric mobility and energy solutions portfolio. Its electric two-wheelers cater to individual customers (B2C) seeking technology-led, sustainable, and cost-efficient urban mobility, with recent offerings such as the S1 Pro+ powered by the indigenously developed 4680 Bharat Cell aimed at improving range, performance, safety, and battery reliability. The company also addresses business and fleet customers (B2B), including last-mile delivery operators, ride-sharing providers, corporate fleets, startups, and enterprises requiring mobility or battery-based applications. Beyond vehicles, Ola Electric has expanded into energy storage through the Ola Shakti portfolio, offering battery energy storage solutions for households, small businesses, commercial facilities, farms, and utility-scale users. These solutions are positioned to support power backup, renewable energy integration, energy monitoring, and lower dependence on conventional power backup systems.
