**3.** **Describe the mechanisms to receive and redress grievances of the community.**

OEM is committed to addressing critical community health challenges, including access to quality healthcare, early disease screening, reduction of high-risk pregnancies, prevention of anaemia and non-communicable diseases, and improvement of nutritional outcomes. Through the Ola Foundation, the Company works closely with communities in Krishnagiri district to strengthen healthcare systems, build community awareness, and create sustainable linkages with public health institutions.

* **Strengthening Frontline Healthcare Systems:** Field Associates collaborate closely with frontline health workers, including ASHAs, ANMs, and Anganwadi Workers, to enhance service delivery. Their efforts include supporting health camps, identifying high-risk cases, facilitating timely referrals, and promoting the effective use of health data to improve community health outcomes.
* **Promoting Community Awareness and Healthcare Access:** The Company actively engages communities through awareness programs focused on nutrition, anaemia, maternal health, menstrual hygiene, and non-communicable diseases. These initiatives are conducted through platforms such as Village Health, Sanitation and Nutrition Days, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), patient support groups, and other community forums. Additionally, the MotoAarogyam initiative leverages technology-enabled healthcare solutions integrated with electric mobility to improve access to basic health services in underserved regions.
* **Creating Sustainable Community Impact:** By fostering collaboration between healthcare systems and local communities, the Company aims to build a more inclusive, responsive, and sustainable healthcare ecosystem. Through continuous engagement and support, these initiatives help bridge gaps in healthcare access and contribute to long-term improvements in community wellbeing
