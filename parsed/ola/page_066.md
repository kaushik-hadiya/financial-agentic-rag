## **Cell Business Review**

### From validation to commercial manufacturing

FY26 marked the transition of the Cell business from technology validation towards commercial manufacturing. The Company commissioned 2.5 GWh of operational capacity, increased Cell output during the year and commercially deployed the 4680 Bharat Cell in its vehicles. By the FY26 results date, the core installation required to reach 6 GWh was largely complete.

The immediate priority is to commission and utilise the 6 GWh platform, improve manufacturing yield and line stability, and deepen own-Cell integration across the Automotive portfolio. Progress will be evaluated through qualified output, quality, yield, cost, utilisation, commercial sales and cash collection rather than nameplate capacity alone.

### NMC integration and LFP readiness

The Company’s Cell roadmap includes complementary NMC and LFP technologies. The 4680 NMC Bharat Cell is commercially deployed and is intended for performance-oriented mobility applications where energy density and power are important. After the Conclusion of FY26, The 46100 LFP Cell received BIS certification and is vehicle-ready, providing a cost-optimised platform for mass-mobility and energy-storage applications.

This technology roadmap enables the Company to select the appropriate chemistry and form factor for each application. NMC Cells can support performance-oriented mobility products, cylindrical LFP Cells can support mass-mobility and distributed storage, and storage-optimised LFP formats can support larger commercial, industrial and utility-scale systems.

### ACC PLI programme

In May 2026, the Ministry of Heavy Industries revised the milestone timeline applicable to Ola Cell Technologies under the Advanced Chemistry Cell PLI programme, providing a full five-year incentive window through CY2031 for its 20 GWh allocation. PLI disbursements are expected to be made quarterly as eligible Cell production and sales scale, subject to domestic value-addition requirements, verification, continuing compliance and approval by the relevant authorities.

The programme can support the economics and cash profile of the manufacturing ramp. However, capital allocation and liquidity planning will continue to be based on prudent assumptions, without relying on the maximum or early receipt of incentives.
