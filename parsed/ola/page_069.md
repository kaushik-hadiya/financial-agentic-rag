## **Capital Allocation and Use of Existing Assets**

The Company has invested substantially in the Futurefactory, the Gigafactory, product platforms, software and research and development through its Battery Innovation Center. The next phase of value creation will depend increasingly on improving the utilisation and productivity of these assets.

Capital allocation will therefore prioritise investments linked to commercially validated demand, safety, quality, maintenance, regulatory compliance and expected returns. Higher throughput through existing infrastructure can improve fixed-cost absorption and cash generation without requiring a proportionate increase in Automotive capital expenditure.

Investment decisions will consider utilisation, contribution, customer impact, working-capital requirements, financing needs and downside scenarios.

### **Internal Controls and Risk Management**

The Board and Management are committed to and responsible for maintaining internal control systems required to scale, given the complexities of the operations. The framework is intended to support efficient operations, safeguard assets, prevent and detect fraud and error, maintain accurate records and enable timely and reliable financial reporting.

The Audit Committee oversees the internal audit plan and reviews the findings and management actions. The risk management committee oversees the enterprise risk management process across strategic, operational, financial, technological and regulatory risks. Business functions are responsible for identifying and managing risks within their activities, supported by common policies, escalation processes and independent assurances, where appropriate.
