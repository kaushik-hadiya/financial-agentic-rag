## **Product Announcements After FY26**

Since the close of FY26, the Company has announced new products and portfolio extensions across both mobility and energy storage. These developments demonstrate how the common technology platform is being translated into products for distinct customer segments.

The Company has identified opportunities of more than 35 GWh through signed, non-binding memoranda of understanding, including an arrangement with Axis Energy for the supply of up to 20 GWh through 2032. These opportunities represent a potential demand pipeline and do not constitute firm purchase orders or committed revenue. The pipeline is expected to support higher utilisation of the Company’s cell-manufacturing platform as production scales.

### **S1Z: bringing indigenous LFP technology to the value segment**

In August 2026, the Company opened bookings for the S1Z, its most accessible scooter range, with an announced starting price of ₹79,999. The S1Z is designed as India's first scooter range powered by Ola's indigenous Bharat Cell LFP technology, developed at the Battery Innovation Centre and manufactured at the Gigafactory in Tamil Nadu.

The announced product offers a range of up to 301 kilometres under IDC testing together with the MoveOS 5 experience. It is also intended to be the first product range scaled through dealer-operated Ola stores. The S1Z therefore brings together three elements of the next phase of the mobility strategy: a lower entry price, in-house LFP integration and broader dealer-led distribution.

### **Extending the energy-storage product portfolio**

The post-year-end product roadmap also broadens the energy-storage portfolio beyond the first Shakti product. Shakti Gen 2 is designed in 5.2 kWh and 9.1 kWh configurations for houses, home backup, larger household loads and rooftop-solar integration. Planned Shakti DC Block, AC Rack and Cluster products in the 50-73.6 kWh range are intended for telecom towers, petrol pumps, quick-commerce dark stores and other distributed commercial sites.

For larger applications, the Mahashakti portfolio includes rack-based products and a 6.26 MWh containerised system for data centres, factories, independent power producers and renewable-energy integration. The current roadmap targets commercial launch of the rack and container products by March 2027.

**The portfolio now connects affordable electric mobility, household storage, distributed infrastructure and large-format energy systems through a common Cell and engineering platform.**
