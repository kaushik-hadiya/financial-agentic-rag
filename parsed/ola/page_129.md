## 12. **Describe the measures taken by the entity to ensure a safe and healthy workplace.**

OEM adopts a proactive approach to health and safety by implementing comprehensive measures aimed at preventing workplace incidents, injuries, occupational illnesses, and lost workdays. Regular safety audits are conducted by qualified internal teams, and employees receive ongoing training on key topics such as chemical safety, fire safety, and process safety. The Company utilizes Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment (HIRA) methodologies to systematically identify workplace hazards, evaluate associated risks, and implement appropriate controls. OEM is committed to maintaining high standards of occupational health and safety and ensuring compliance with applicable regulatory requirements, including ISO 45001.

To strengthen its safety culture, OEM has integrated multiple preventive measures across its operations. Infrastructure and equipment are designed with built-in safety features, including automated shutdown mechanisms, detection systems, containment measures, and suppression systems. A Permit-to-Work system is in place to manage contractor activities and non-routine tasks, while periodic safety inspections and workplace assessments help identify and address unsafe conditions and behaviors. HIRA studies are regularly conducted for routine operations to ensure effective risk controls are maintained. In addition, the Company implements an annual EHS training program for employees, conducts scheduled workplace inspections, and undertakes periodic medical examinations for employees and contract workers. Health and wellness camps are also organized to promote employee wellbeing and support a safe and healthy working environment.
