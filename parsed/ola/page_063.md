## **Financial Performance Commentary**

### Revenue, Volume and Margin Performance

FY26 deliveries were 173,787 units compared with 359,221 units in FY25, while consolidated revenue from operations declined from ₹4,514 crore to ₹2,253 crore. The contraction reflected weaker demand momentum and the impact of the service, distribution and operating-model changes undertaken during the year.

Despite the lower revenue base, consolidated gross margin % increased by 12.7 percentage points to 30.6%. Gross profit declined from ₹806 crore to ₹690 crore, a reduction of only 14.4% compared with the 50.1% decline in revenue. This reflected stronger unit economics supported by the Gen 3 platform, vertical integration, pricing architecture, lower warranty costs and cost optimisation. Q4 FY26 consolidated gross margin reached 38.5%, or 33.5% excluding PLI income.

Gross margin will continue to be evaluated alongside volume, contribution and cash conversion. Its sustainability remains dependent on product mix, pricing, commodity costs, localisation, manufacturing absorption, incentive eligibility and warranty performance.

### Operating Expenditure, Profitability and Cash Flow

Consolidated operating expenditure declined from ₹2,545 crore in FY25 to ₹1,682 crore in FY26. The adjusted operating EBITDA loss narrowed from ₹1,903 crore to ₹1,203 crore, while the loss after tax narrowed from ₹2,276 crore to ₹1,833 crore. The adjusted operating EBITDA margin remained affected by the lower revenue base, underscoring the importance of rebuilding productive volumes while maintaining the reset cost structure.

On a quarterly basis, OPEX reduced from ₹779 crore in Q4 FY25 to ₹451 crore in Q1 FY26 and ₹416 crore in Q2 FY26. Expenditure increased modestly to ₹432 crore in Q3 FY26 during the intensive operating-reset and service-stabilisation phase, before declining to ₹383 crore in Q4 FY26. This represents a reduction of approximately 51% compared with Q4 FY25 and 15% through FY26 from Q1 to Q4.
