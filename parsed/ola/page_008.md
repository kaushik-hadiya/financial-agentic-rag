## **The Advantage behind the scale**

### A Manufacturing Platform That Compounds

**A manufacturing platform designed for speed, flexibility and structural cost advantage.**

The FutureFactory is designed as a flexible manufacturing platform - connecting in-house technology, common product architectures and vertically integrated manufacturing to support multiple products without rebuilding the manufacturing base each time

#### **Integrated by Design**

In-house ownership of core technologies enables product engineering and manufacturing to evolve together - improving control over quality, cost and execution.

#### **Flexible by Architecture**

Modular platforms allow the same manufacturing ecosystem to serve different products, segments and price points - helping accelerate time to market.

#### **Efficient at Scale**

Platform commonality, vertical integration and localised sourcing create structural cost advantages while supporting capital-efficient growth.

Deep vertical integration combined with localised sourcing helps reduce dependencies across critical components and improves control over the manufacturing value chain
