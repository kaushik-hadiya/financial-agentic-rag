## **Ushering in a cultural renaissance**

### Preserving knowledge while making heritage relevant to new generations

Culture is a source of identity, dignity and continuity. Ola Foundation seeks to preserve and activate India's cultural knowledge by making heritage accessible, participative and relevant to younger generations. During the year, the Foundation supported more than 20 cultural-preservation projects, organised over 50 intergenerational knowledge sessions and supported 14 students through the Jadunath Sarkar Fellowships. Cultural storytelling and traditional knowledge were also integrated into community health, nutrition and education programmes.

The Foundation's Culture and Heritage agenda is being developed across four areas: heritage promotion through technology; storytelling as a narrative bridge; research and innovation; and government partnerships and community engagement. Together, these pillars encompass digitisation, interactive experiences, community-created narratives, scholarships, art residencies, research grants, collaborative programmes and heritage trails.

### **PARTNERSHIPS THAT DEEPEN IMPACT**

Ola Foundation collaborates with government bodies, research institutions and civil-society organisations across public health, livelihoods, mental well-being, cultural preservation, conservation and research. Its partner ecosystem includes the Department of Public Health and Preventive Medicine, Government of Tamil Nadu; the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship; the Foundation for Indian Historical and Cultural Research; the Centre for Wildlife Studies; Nirvikalpa Integrated Therapy Foundation; Live Love Laugh; the Immunology and Arthritis Research and Education Trust; and IIT Bombay.
