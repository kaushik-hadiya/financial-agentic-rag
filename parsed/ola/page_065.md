## Distribution and installed-base opportunity

The FY26 network reset shifted the emphasis from the number of locations to the productivity, relevance and service capability of each market. The next phase combines the Company’s digital capabilities with qualified dealer partners that can provide local reach, customer relationships and market knowledge. More than 1,200 indications of interest were received following the dealer-partner announcement after year-end; these represent potential partners and not operational outlets.

Subsequent to the year-end, the dealer-partner model progressed from initial interest to execution. After opening its sales and service network to dealer partners in August 2026, the Company launched its first cohort of dealer-operated stores within a month, covering cities and towns across Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Bihar, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh. The Company is targeting a network of more than 500 dealerships over the next couple of quarters, while its existing company-owned stores transition into brand and product experience centres. The partner-led model is intended to deepen local-market penetration, improve customer accessibility and enable faster response across geographies.

Our installed base exceeded one million customers after year-end. As this base matures, parts, paid repairs, maintenance, accessories, software and other post-warranty services can create opportunities for recurring revenue and deeper customer engagement. Development of these offerings will remain dependent on reliable core service, transparent customer value and appropriate controls over customer data and privacy.
