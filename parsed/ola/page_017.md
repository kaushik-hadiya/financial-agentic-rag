## **Bharat Cell Moves from Validation to Commercial Scale**

FY26 marked the transition of the 4680 Bharat Cell from development and validation to commercial deployment. The Company commissioned 2.5 GWh of operational Cell capacity, integrated its own Cells into vehicles and largely completed the installation required for the 6 GWh platform.

The immediate priority is to commission and utilise the 6 GWh platform, improve yields and line stability, and deepen own-Cell integration across the vehicle portfolio. The technology roadmap combines 4680 NMC for performance applications with 46100 LFP for more affordable mobility and energy-storage applications.

### **Q1: Readiness.**
Cells were ready for phased integration into vehicles. The strategic intent was clear: replace supplier cells progressively with in-house 4680 cells, deepen control over cost and supply, and use captive auto demand to de-risk the manufacturing ramp.

### **Q2: Gigawatt-scale operation.**
The Gigafactory commissioned 2.5 GWh capacity and the first 4680-powered vehicles reached customers. This turned cell manufacturing from a future capability into an operating industrial asset.

### **Q3: Commercial deployment.**
Cell output doubled sequentially to 72,418 units and in-house 4680 cells entered commercial vehicle deployment. The same output also powered the first pure-play energy product, Shakti.

### **Q4: Scale platform.**
Operational capacity stood at 2.5 GWh and installation towards 6 GWh was completed. The roadmap broadened across NMC for performance applications, LFP for mass mobility and distributed storage, and prismatic LFP for large-format BESS.

**The cell is no longer only a component. It is becoming the common technology platform across mobility and energy.**
