As part of our commitment to continual improvement, Energy Conservation (EnCon) initiatives have been systematically planned and implemented at the OLA Future Factory. Key initiatives for FY 2025-26 include

### **1. Electricity Conservation:**

Green energy sourcing from **Wind energy** has been implemented, resulting in utilisation of 5,819,896 units of green energy. Wind energy is a **non-fossil, renewable source of energy** that generates electricity without the direct combustion of coal, oil, or other fossil fuels. Its utilisation contributes to reducing dependence on conventional energy sources and supports the transition towards a cleaner and more sustainable energy mix.

The increased use of wind energy helps in **reducing greenhouse gas emissions and lowering the organisation's overall carbon footprint.** It also supports the efficient utilisation of renewable natural resources while contributing to long-term environmental sustainability.

The utilisation of **5,819,896 units of wind-generated green energy** demonstrates our continued efforts towards adopting cleaner energy solutions, improving environmental performance, and supporting the transition to a **low-carbon and sustainable energy future.**

* Electricity consumption was successfully reduced during FY 2025-26 through the implementation of targeted energy conservation initiatives. Key measures contributing to this reduction include:
* Power reduction in supply and exhaust fans – This helped to reduce power consumption by 13,091 units per month.
* Operating compressors at lower operating pressure for energy saving – This helped to achieve a power saving of 9,660 units per month.
* Operating a single VFD compressor during non-production days/hours – This resulted in significant savings of 920 units/month.
* Optimization of shop floor lightings during Non Production Days/night hours – This resulted in a saving of 1,512 units per month.
* Optimization of peripheral lighting – This resulted in a saving of 1,227 units per month.
* Optimization of testing lab air conditioning systems based on application and usage – This resulted in a saving of 850 units per month.
* Switching off machine conveyors during idle time – This resulted in a saving of 2,160 units per month.
* Paint Jig stripping process technology has been changed to ambient stripping technology instead of hot stripping. This gives an energy saving of 68212 units per month.
