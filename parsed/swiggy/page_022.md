### **Supply Chain and Distribution**

## **Empowering the Ecosystem at Scale**

**Behind every fast-moving marketplace is a supply chain that keeps things moving. Our Supply Chain and Distribution business supports wholesalers and retailers with integrated procurement, warehousing, logistics and fulfilment capabilities, helping make the ecosystem faster, sharper and more reliable.**

During the year, the business scaled its B2B supply chain services while strengthening warehousing capabilities and expanding value-added services for partners. Revenue growth remained healthy, supported by rising throughput and deeper partner engagement, while the focus stayed on improving operating efficiency as the business scaled.
