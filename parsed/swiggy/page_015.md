## **Convenience that Compounds**

**Every new user, restaurant, dark store and delivery partner **strengthens the platform for everyone else. This creates a self-**reinforcing loop: more demand attracts more supply, stronger** **supply improves selection and reliability, and better experiences** **bring users back more often. As our services overlap across cities** **and use cases, the platform becomes faster, smarter and more** deeply embedded into everyday life.**
