## **Championing Social Equity and Delivery Partner Welfare**

True sustainability extends beyond environmental metrics, it is rooted in social responsibility and the long-term well-being of the people who power our platform.

* Swiggy signed the **World Economic Forum (WEF) Good Work Principles Charter at Davos**, actively contributing to global benchmarks that champion platform economy best practices. From physical safety to financial resilience, we are building a more equitable and resilient future for millions of our delivery partners through a comprehensive, human-centric ecosystem

* Prioritising well-being on the road, we scaled our safety initiatives in FY 2025-26 by conducting **comprehensive road safety workshops** nationwide, reinforcing defensive driving and protective protocols

* We have disbursed over **INR 21 Crores as insurance claims**, backed by a 96% settlement rate within 15 days. Our robust safety net includes Loss of Pay coverage for illnesses up to 13 weeks, unlimited specialist teleconsultations for partners and their dependents, and **15-minute emergency ambulance response** across India

* Transitioning from reactive care to proactive wellness, our specialised **Medical Camps** across major metros (Bengaluru, Mumbai, and New Delhi) successfully screened over 1,000 partners with standardised health diagnostics

* We have democratised financial security by offering **paperless personal loans** directly through the delivery partner application. Furthermore, in partnership with Dvara Money, we launched an innovative **Digital Gold Savings Program**, incentivised by a limited time offer program offering a 1% top-up from Swiggy and high-value lucky draws, empowering our partners to build secure, sustainable wealth for their futures

* We partnered with Sulabh International to map and provide sanitation facilities for our delivery partners. Demonstrating substantial utility and reach, **over 100,000 Delivery Partners** utilised in-app directions to access these sanitised rest spaces

* Our **Financial Literacy Program, driven through workshops led by National Stock Exchange (NSE)**, is a strategic initiative designed to equip Delivery Partners with competencies in capital allocation, debt optimisation, and diversified micro-investments, driving measurable long-term financial resilience and accelerated professional advancement

## **Advancing Gender Equity and Inclusive Growth**

At Swiggy, we view diversity not merely as a metric, but as a catalyst for profound socio-economic growth. By intentionally breaking down structural barriers in traditionally male-dominated spaces, we are creating an ecosystem where women can achieve financial independence, and thrive in a safe and supportive environment. Our commitment to embedding equity across our value chain is driven by clear, actionable pillars:

* We are running pilot programs to onboard women delivery partners. Through our pilot with the **Department of Women and Child Development (DWCD)**, we transition women from Self-Help Groups (SHGs) into flexible earning roles on our platform. By streamlining onboarding and dismantling gender-specific barriers, this initiative unlocks new independent income streams for women

* Fostering diversity is an active operational mandate within our quick commerce network. Women now comprise an average of **20% of our warehouse associate workforce**, with several cities leading the charge at 40%

* We invest in workplace safety to ensure all employees have a secure, supportive environment to grow. In FY 2025-26, we provided comprehensive **self-defense training to over 120 women employees across six major cities**, ensuring that personal safety and well-being remain non-negotiable pillars of our organisation

* Celebrating the spirit of female enterprise, we hosted the second edition of **She The Change: Celebrating Women Chefs and Entrepreneurs**. The event honored the remarkable drive and market contributions of over 28 exceptional women culinary entrepreneurs on our platform, actively fueling the growth of women-led micro-businesses

By combining safety and financial independence, Swiggy is working towards building an equitable workforce for women.
