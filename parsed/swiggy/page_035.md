## **Company Overview**

Swiggy Limited (hereafter referred to as “Swiggy” or “the Company”) is India’s pioneering on-demand convenience platform, transforming the way consumers access food and everyday essentials. Swiggy offers a range of user-friendly services that enable customers to browse, select, order and pay for food, groceries and household essentials, with deliveries fulfilled through its on-demand delivery partner network.

Established in 2013, the Company was founded with a mission to enhance the quality of life for urban consumers by providing seamless access to daily essentials. Over the years, Swiggy has emerged as a leading player in India’s online food delivery market by leveraging robust technology capabilities, an extensive delivery network and a customer-centric operating model.

Swiggy has progressively diversified its offerings beyond food delivery to include Swiggy Instamart, its quick commerce platform for groceries and household essentials, and Swiggy Dineout, which offers restaurant discovery, table reservations and dining-related experiences. The platform partners with hundreds of thousands of restaurants, retail merchants, brand partners and delivery executives across hundreds of cities, with a focus on enabling reliable and convenient hyperlocal commerce experiences.

Backed by leading global and domestic investors, Swiggy continues to focus on innovation, operational efficiency and customer experience across its platform ecosystem. The Company remains focussed on strengthening its position within India’s rapidly evolving digital consumption and hyperlocal delivery landscape.

## **Diversified Offerings**

### **Food Delivery**

icon: food delivery

Swiggy’s Food Delivery business remains the cornerstone of its platform ecosystem, offering a comprehensive on-demand food delivery service that connects customers with a wide selection of restaurant partners through its app and website. The overall platform service currently operates across 720+ cities in India and caters to approximately 18.3 million Monthly Transacting Users (MTUs). In addition to order fulfilment, the platform also provides advertising and visibility solutions to restaurant partners, supporting customer discovery and engagement across the network.

In FY 2025-26, the Food Delivery segment delivered steady growth, with Gross Order Value (GOV) increasing by 20.2% year-on-year (YoY) to INR 34,593 Crores, while MTUs increased by 19.2% to 17.5 million.

### **Quick Commerce**

icon: quick commerce

Swiggy’s Quick Commerce business, Instamart, focuses on rapid delivery of groceries and household essentials through a dense hyperlocal fulfilment network. Since its launch in 2020, Instamart has expanded its presence to 125+ cities, supported by a network of 1,143 active dark stores across India. Backed by dedicated delivery infrastructure, the platform has reduced delivery timelines to approximately 13 minutes.

Over time, the business has expanded beyond grocery-led use cases to include a wider assortment across non-grocery categories, resulting in larger dark-store formats and broader SKU availability. Instamart has also introduced larger-format dark stores, referred to as “megapods”, spanning approximately 8-10k sq. ft., capable of housing up to 50k SKUs.

In FY 2025-26, the Quick Commerce segment witnessed strong scale-up, with GOV increasing by 94.1% YoY to INR 28,496 Crores and MTUs rising by 73.7% to 12.3 million. Average Order Value (AOV) increased by 34.4% YoY to INR 691, supported by broader assortment availability and increasing consumer adoption. During the year, the Company added 122 stores, resulting in a significant increase in active dark-store area to approximately 4.8 million sq. ft., representing growth of 21.1% YoY.

## **Out-of-Home Consumption**

icon: location pin

Swiggy’s Out-of-Home consumption segment has two primary services, Swiggy Dineout and Swiggy Scenes. Dineout offers a platform for users to discover restaurants and make reservations, while Scenes is a dedicated platform for booking in-restaurant events, enhancing the Company’s presence in dining-related services beyond food delivery. The services leverage Swiggy’s consumer base and tech capabilities, cross-pollinating users and brand salience for a stronger relationship with both users and restaurant partners. Dineout was one of the few acquisitions by Swiggy in its journey and was integrated into its unified app a couple of years back. These services strengthen Swiggy’s presence in the lifestyle and dining space, catering to the evolving preferences of urban consumers seeking premium dining and entertainment options.

In FY 2025-26, the Out-of-Home Consumption segment continued to scale and move towards profitability, with GOV increasing by 50.6% YoY to INR 4,645 Crores, while Monthly Active Restaurants increased by 30.2% to approximately 47k.

## **Supply Chain and Distribution**

icon: supply chain

Swiggy, through its subsidiary Swiggy Networks (Scootsy), provides supply chain and distribution solutions focused on warehousing, fulfilment and retail distribution for brands, wholesalers and retailers. The business offers integrated warehousing services, including inventory management, in-warehouse processing and packaging solutions, alongside distribution capabilities supported by Swiggy’s logistics network. Swiggy Networks (Scootsy) also partners with brands to support retail expansion and improve distribution reach across markets. By leveraging technology-enabled fulfilment and logistics capabilities, the business aims to improve operational efficiency and delivery reliability across the supply chain ecosystem.

## **Platform Innovations**

icon: platform innovations

As a technology-led platform, Swiggy continues to build, test and scale new offerings aligned with its focus on convenience-led consumption. These include adjacent businesses as well as emerging platform opportunities across hyperlocal commerce and digital services. Recent initiatives include Toing, an experimental marketplace designed to explore affordable food delivery and expand category penetration among value-conscious consumers. Swiggy also introduced Crew, a concierge-style assistance platform that helps users plan, coordinate and simplify everyday tasks and experiences, extending convenience beyond transactions.
