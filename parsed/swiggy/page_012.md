### **Bolt: Food That Keeps Pace With the Moment**

Bolt took the idea of quick food delivery from a convenience feature to a mainstream behaviour. Live across 700+ cities and contributing over 1 in 10 food delivery orders, Bolt helped customers access fast-prep meals in minutes. It also strengthened Swiggy’s salience for impulse, in-between and time-sensitive food occasions.

### **Express Delivery: Faster Access, Wider Availability**

Express Delivery was extended beyond Swiggy One BLCK, allowing more users to opt into faster delivery through an additional charge. This created a sharper speed-led choice for customers who want priority fulfilment, without limiting the experience to a premium subscription base.

### **Instamart: Speed With Broader Utility**

Instamart continued to strengthen its promise of fast, reliable delivery while expanding what customers could buy. With larger stores, denser networks and megapods, speed became more than a grocery advantage. It became the foundation for quick commerce across many more missions.
