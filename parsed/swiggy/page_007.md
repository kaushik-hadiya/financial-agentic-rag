### **Chairman's Message**

## **Building for India's Next Consumption Era**

**Dear Shareholders,**

It gives me great pleasure to write to you at an important stage in Swiggy's journey. FY 2025-26 was our first full year as a listed company. It marked the beginning of a more mature chapter, where scale, ambition and innovation were matched by stronger discipline, greater transparency and a deeper focus on long-term value creation. The progress achieved during the year reaffirms the strength of your Company's business model and the clarity of its strategy.

India entered the year on a strong economic footing. Despite global uncertainty, geopolitical tensions and uneven growth across major economies, the Indian economy continued to show resilience. Growth was supported by domestic consumption, expanding services activity, infrastructure investment, easing inflation and improving real incomes. Household consumption remained central to India's growth story, with private final consumption expenditure accounting for its highest share of GDP in over a decade.

photo: Anand Kripalu

This backdrop is significant for Swiggy. Your Company operates at the intersection of consumption, technology and logistics. As India's digital economy expands, consumers are becoming more comfortable with app-led commerce, and convenience is moving from being a premium proposition to an everyday expectation. India's retail market remains digitally underpenetrated. Quick commerce is still early in its evolution. Food delivery continues to have meaningful headroom as more consumers and meal occasions move online. Dining out, events, household essentials, personal care and other everyday needs are all being reshaped by digital access, reliable fulfilment and wider choice.

Swiggy was built for this shift. Over the years, your Company has not merely participated in emerging categories; it has helped create and shape them. From food delivery to quick commerce, from dining out to newer convenience-led services, Swiggy has designed solutions around real, everyday consumer needs. The purpose has remained simple yet powerful: to elevate the quality of life of urban consumers by offering unparalleled convenience.

During the year, this purpose translated into broad-based progress across the platform. Food Delivery continued to deepen engagement by expanding the range of occasions and consumer needs it serves. Quick Commerce scaled in a large and competitive market, while strengthening its proposition as a broader everyday convenience platform. Out-of-Home Consumption delivered its first full year of profitability, showing how adjacent businesses can become meaningful contributors when integrated well. Across the platform, increasing cross-pollination continued to reinforce the strength of Swiggy's integrated ecosystem.

The Board recognises that leadership in fast-growing sectors requires staying power, financial prudence, robust governance and consumer confidence. This philosophy guided our approach to capital deployment. During the year, Swiggy strengthened its balance sheet through the successful Qualified Institutional Placement of INR 10,000 Crores. Along with the monetisation of the Rapido investment, this timely infusion gives your Company the strategic flexibility to invest for the future, navigate competitive intensity and pursue long-term value creation with confidence.

Technology and innovation have always been fundamental to Swiggy's culture. They allow us to solve evolving consumer needs, improve partner experience and make convenience more reliable, accessible and scalable. What gives the Board confidence is not experimentation alone, but the quality of execution, learning and accountability behind it. As Swiggy grows, this balance between ambition and rigour will remain critical.

Responsible growth is equally central to the Company's future. NSE Sustainability Ratings & Analytics has placed the Company in the 'Leader' category, reflecting Swiggy's performance across environment, social and governance parameters. The Company also continues to advance its 2030 Sustainability Goals, anchored in Climate, Community and Conscience. These include commitments such as achieving a 100% EV delivery fleet, transitioning to 100% renewable energy in direct operations, strengthening circular packaging practices, upskilling and engaging 1 million individuals in the value chain, empowering 100,000 women across the ecosystem, improving delivery partner welfare and strengthening governance and data privacy.

Governance remains a key priority for the Board. The transition to public markets has strengthened Swiggy's commitment to transparency, disclosure quality, independent oversight and stakeholder engagement. Our aim is to build a company that not only meets the expectations of a listed enterprise, but earns confidence consistently as an institution operating at the centre of India's evolving digital consumption economy.

As Swiggy enters its next phase, our priorities are clear: prudent capital allocation, continued technology investments, sharper differentiation and steady progress towards profitability. The journey so far has been exciting, but the future offers an even larger canvas. We will use the foundation built over the years, and the lessons learned along the way, to strengthen our businesses, deepen customer understanding, maintain cost discipline and create sustainable value.

On behalf of the Board, I thank our shareholders for their continued confidence in Swiggy. I also acknowledge the dedication of the management team and employees, and the trust of our customers, delivery partners, restaurant partners, merchant partners and brand partners, whose collective contribution continues to strengthen the Company's platform and purpose.

Swiggy's next chapter will be shaped by the same conviction that has brought us here: to keep building with purpose, serving with care and creating an institution worthy of India's next consumption era.

Warm regards,

**Anand Kripalu**
Chairperson
Swiggy Limited
