**Dear Shareholders,**

FY 2025-26 was a year in which we challenged the conventional narrative for a newly listed company. We increased our innovation quotient, strengthened execution across businesses and focussed on building the key blocks of our growth trajectory. At Swiggy, the year reinforced a belief that has shaped us from the beginning: consumer-backward innovation and capital-efficient execution at scale remain the most durable recipe for success.

At the platform level, Business-to-Consumer Gross Order Value (B2C GOV) grew 45.5% year-on-year to INR 67,734 Crores, supported by 1.14 billion B2C orders, up 23.6%, and average Monthly Transacting Users (MTUs) of 23.5 million, up 33.1%. Consolidated Adjusted Revenue increased 48.9% to INR 24,315 Crores. More users engaged with multiple Swiggy services during the year, strengthening cross-pollination across the ecosystem and making the platform relevant across more everyday missions.

Equally important, the year strengthened our confidence in the operating architecture of the business. Food Delivery accelerated while improving profitability. Instamart scaled rapidly in a highly competitive category while making progress on unit economics. Out-of-Home Consumption delivered its first full year of profitability. Platform Innovations served as a structured space to test new propositions.

#### **Food Delivery: Expanding the Category, Profitably**

Food Delivery had a strong year, with GOV growing 20.2% to INR 34,593 Crores. Orders increased 13.6% to 714 million, while average MTUs grew 19.2% to 17.5 million. Average Order Value (AOV) moved from INR 458 to INR 484, reflecting a healthy balance of premiumisation and affordability-led propositions. Adjusted EBITDA crossed INR 1,000 Crores, up 76.7%, with Adjusted EBITDA margin expanding to 2.9% of GOV. Contribution margin improved to 7.5%, supported by advertising growth, better fleet utilisation and operating leverage.

The year also validated our view that Food Delivery has meaningful headroom in India. We built propositions across speed, selection, accessibility and value. Bolt and One BLCK addressed time-sensitive needs. 99 Store made the platform more accessible for value-conscious users. EatRight gained traction among consumers looking for healthier choices. DeskEats and Food on Train helped expand the platform into workplace and travel-linked occasions. These initiatives collectively created more reasons for consumers to use Swiggy and helped expand the addressable market.

The category can continue to expand if we keep solving sharper use cases and work with restaurant partners to improve affordability, reliability and choice.
