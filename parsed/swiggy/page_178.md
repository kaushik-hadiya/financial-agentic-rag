### **31 Employment benefit plans**

#### **(a) Defined contribution plan**

The Company makes contributions to provident fund and pension fund which are defined contribution plan for qualifying employees. Under the schemes, the Company is required to contribute a specified percentage of the payroll costs to fund the benefits. The Company recognized ₹ 22 Crore (March 31, 2025: ₹ 19 Crore) for provident fund and pension fund in the standalone statement of profit and loss.

#### **(b) Defined benefit plan**

The Company offers Gratuity benefit to employees, a defined benefit plan, Gratuity plan is governed in accordance with the Code on Social Security, 2020. The Company's gratuity plan is unfunded and provides for a lump sum payment to vested employees at retirement, death while in employment or on termination of employment of an amount equivalent to 15 days wages payable as defined under the Code on Wages, 2019 for each completed year of service. Vesting occurs upon completion of contractual period of continuous years of service as defined in Code on Social Security 2020.
