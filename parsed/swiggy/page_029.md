### **Glossary**

## **Decoded: The Swiggy Playbook**

#### **1. Swiggy Platform**

<table><tr><th><strong>Term</strong></th><th><strong>Description</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>B2C Total Orders</strong></td><td>Consolidated completed orders of consumer facing businesses i.e. (i) Food delivery, plus (ii) Quick Commerce, plus (iii) Out-of-Home Consumption, excluding Platform Innovations</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>B2C Gross Order Value (GOV)</strong></td><td>Consolidated Gross Order Value of completed order for consumer facing businesses i.e. (i) Food Delivery, plus (ii) Quick Commerce, plus (iii) Out-of-Home Consumption, excluding Platform Innovations</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>B2C Adjusted EBITDA</strong></td><td>Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA of (i) Food Delivery, plus (ii) Quick Commerce, plus (iii) Out-of-Home Consumption, excluding Platform Innovations</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>B2C Adjusted EBITDA Margin (% of B2C GOV)</strong></td><td>B2C Adjusted EBITDA divided by B2C GOV</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Consolidated Revenue</strong></td><td>Consolidated Revenue from operations as per financials of all businesses i.e. (i) Food Delivery, plus (ii) Quick Commerce, plus (iii) Out-of-Home Consumption, plus (iv) Supply Chain and Distribution, plus (v) Platform Innovations</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Consolidated Gross Revenue</strong></td><td>Consolidated Gross Revenue of all businesses i.e. (i) Food Delivery, plus (ii) Quick Commerce, plus (iii) Out-of-Home Consumption, plus (iv) Supply Chain and Distribution, plus (v) Platform Innovations</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Consolidated EBITDA</strong></td><td>Profit/loss as per financials excluding (i) tax expense (ii) depreciation and amortisation expense (iii) finance cost</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA</strong></td><td>Consolidated EBITDA excluding (i) other income (ii) exceptional items (iii) share in net loss of an associate (iv) share based payment expense and (v) rental expenses pertaining to ‘Ind AS 116’ leases</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Average Monthly Transacting Users</strong></td><td>Number of unique transacting users that have completed at least one order on the Swiggy unified-app / website in a month, averaged for the months in the period/year</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Average Monthly Transacting Delivery Partners</strong></td><td>Number of unique delivery partners that have delivered at least one order in a month, averaged for the months in the period/year</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Platform Frequency</strong></td><td>Completed orders per user in a month, averaged for the months in the period/year</td></tr></table>

#### **2. Food Delivery**

<table><tr><th><strong>Term</strong></th><th><strong>Description</strong></th></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Total Orders</strong></td><td>Total completed Food Delivery orders on the platform</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Gross Order Value (GOV)</strong></td><td>Total monetary value of completed Food Delivery orders (gross of any discounts) plus (i) user delivery charges (net of any discounts, including free delivery discounts provided for Swiggy One membership program), plus (ii) packaging charges, plus (iii) fee from users, plus (iv) taxes, excluding tips.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Average Order Value (AOV)</strong></td><td>Food Delivery GOV divided by Food Delivery Total Orders</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Revenue</strong></td><td>Revenue from our Food Delivery business includes (i) pre-agreed commissions from restaurant partners; (ii) advertising revenue from restaurant partners; (iii) fees charged to users and delivery partners for the use of our technology platform and subscription revenue (net of discounts, credits and refunds other than free delivery); and (iv) fees for other business enablement services from restaurant partners</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Gross Revenue</strong></td><td>Revenue plus (i) user delivery charges collected and passed on to delivery partners (net of any discounts, including free delivery discounts provided through Swiggy One membership program), plus (ii) fee from users collected and netted off from platform funded discounts given for corresponding orders</td></tr></table>
