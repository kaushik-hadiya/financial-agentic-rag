### **Enabling Zero-Emission Deliveries**

EV adoption is central to our sustainability goals and we are building the infrastructure to make it accessible for our delivery partners and democratise access to green mobility.

* **Diverse Rental Choices:** Delivery partners can choose from over **60+ EV models** during onboarding, enabling a transition to green mobility
* **EV Giveaways & Incentives:** To directly foster ownership, and reward our fleet, we have launched **targeted EV giveaway campaigns**, placing zero-emission vehicles directly into the hands of our partners to accelerate EV adoption

Driven by these initiatives, our EV fleet has **doubled since FY 2024-25**, marking a massive **14x jump** from our original baseline and successfully avoiding over **13,300 tCO<sub>2</sub>e** emissions in FY 2025-26.

These initiatives form the core of our environmental impact strategy, leveraging technology to make every delivery count. Our **Eco-Saver** feature uses intelligent routing to optimise delivery paths, eliminating **8.9 million kilometres** of travel and preventing **812.6 metric tonnes of CO<sub>2</sub>e emissions** in FY 2025-26 alone.

As a founding member of the **UNEP Deliver-E Coalition**, a global, industry-led alliance launched by major delivery platforms, Swiggy is spearheading the transition to sustainable last mile deliveries. We are aggressively driving the adoption of electric two wheelers across our operations.

#### **Renewable Infrastructure & Ecosystem-Led Innovation**

We have successfully installed solar power systems across **7 major warehouses, achieving a total capacity of 2.2 MW.** This structural shift to clean energy has resulted in an annual reduction of 3,000 metric tonnes of CO<sub>2</sub> emissions.

A vibrant marketplace of eco-friendly alternatives can accelerate systemic climate action. Recognising this, we partnered with 35 startups to enable EV adoption and sustainable packaging, through our dedicated packaging platform, supporting early-stage green ventures.
