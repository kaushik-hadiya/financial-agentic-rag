## **Strengthening Food Security & Safety and Enabling India's Culinary Heritage**

Swiggy leverages its platform architecture to make growth seamless for restaurant partners and enable food security. With hyperlocal discovery mechanisms, we actively promote India's rich culinary legacy.

* In FY 2025-26 alone, we distributed **225,995 meals** to underserved populations. Operating in collaboration with Haldirams and the Robin Hood Army, this initiative deploys rapid redistribution logistics to channel refreshments to marginalised communities in Nagpur, Indore, and Raipur

* Leveraging our platform's hyperlocal discovery mechanics, we promote India's rich culinary heritage. By backing traditional and evolving food preferences, unique hyperlocal cuisines, such as Pahari, Bihari, and Goan, we are ensuring that regional culinary identities remain economically viable and vibrant

## **Building a Sustainable Digital Ecosystem**

Swiggy leverages technology as a primary enabler of eco-efficiency using platform architecture, and behavioral design to mitigate climate impact. By altering default choices, optimising digital infrastructure, and establishing marketplace incentives for sustainable materials, we drive circular economy principles across our entire commercial value chain:

* As part of our commitment to responsible tech stewardship, optimising our digital infrastructure and hosting our cloud services on AWS enabled us to avoid over **1,300 MtCO<sub>2</sub>e in carbon emissions** during FY 2025-26, yielding an environmental impact equivalent to the carbon absorbed by more than 21,000 trees growing for a decade

* Redesigning ordering environments can accelerate sustainable consumer choices at scale. By implementing a "no cutlery" default for all food delivery orders, requiring users to actively opt in rather than opt out, we leveraged behavioral nudges to achieve **more than 96% user adoption** **rate.** This policy-aligned intervention drastically minimised single-use plastic waste and avoided **4,797 tonnes of CO<sub>2</sub>e** in FY 2025-26 alone, the ecological equivalent of planting a forest of 218,000 mature trees

* To facilitate sustainable packaging adoption among restaurant partners by 2030, we launched a dedicated Responsible Packaging Website. This digital B2B platform bridges the gap between environmental responsibility and commercial viability, generating **over 2,600 partner leads.** By securing exclusive 10-15% discounts from verified sustainable vendors on eco-friendly SKUs, our technology ensures that responsible choices are economically optimised for small and medium enterprises (SMEs)

## **Excellence in Corporate Governance**

Swiggy maintains a multi-tiered ESG governance framework, with direct oversight from the Board-level Sustainability and CSR Committee, ensuring the strategic integration of environmental, social, and governance priorities.

Furthermore, our robust risk management approach includes continuous identification and prioritisation of critical risks, such as Data Privacy and Cyber Security, and a comprehensive risk assessment for bribery and corruption conducted at least every three years. All identified risks are managed through documented mitigation and contingency plans.

We uphold a highly robust cybersecurity and privacy posture through a Unified Control Framework, integrating ISO 27001 (ISMS) and ISO 27701 (PIMS) certifications. This integrated governance satisfies stringent requirements like PCI-DSS and Data Localisation, ensuring customer data is secure, private, and stored within required geographic boundaries.

Our comprehensive program is reinforced by mandatory employee training, regular audits, and direct Board-level oversight.
