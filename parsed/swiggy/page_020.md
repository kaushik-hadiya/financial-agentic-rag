### **Quick Commerce**

## **Bigger Baskets. Broader Missions.**

**Quick Commerce scaled rapidly while improving its economics, as Instamart strengthened its position from a fast grocery and essentials platform into an everyday convenience destination across categories and occasions.**

#### **The Year that Was**

The business delivered strong GOV growth of 94.1% YoY to INR 28,496 Crores and NOV growth of 699% YoY to INR 20,210 Crores, with Average Monthly Transacting Users rising 73.7% YoY to 12.3 million. Average Order Value increased 34.4% YoY to INR 691, driven by larger baskets and rising non-grocery adoption.

Profitability improved steadily despite heightened competition. Contribution margin improved to (2.8%) of GOV, while Adjusted EBITDA margin stood at (12.3%) of GOV, supported by better basket quality, improving dark store utilisation and operating leverage across fulfilment costs.

#### **Beyond Grocery**

Non-grocery categories emerged as a major growth driver during the year, with increasing traction across electronics, home and kitchen, toys, accessories, gifting and seasonal purchases. Instamart also strengthened its positioning as an “Everything Store” through Quick India Movement (QIM), the first large-format sale event by a quick commerce platform, designed to showcase class-leading selection beyond staples and essentials.

The platform’s assortment strategy remained a key differentiator, centred around hyperlocal selection, unique brand partnerships and expanding general merchandise offerings. Larger-format dark stores and megapods further strengthened category depth and fulfilment efficiency, while the platform maintained industry-leading speed across markets.

#### **Building Larger Baskets**

Basket expansion remained central to improving long-term platform economics and consumer engagement.

Maxxsaver continued scaling through its “cart-within-a-cart” proposition, encouraging larger carts and stronger repeat behaviour. Alongside this, higher non-grocery adoption improved attach rates and helped consumers combine grocery, household and general merchandise purchases within the same order journey.

As a result, Average Order Value rose to INR 691, while GOV growth outpaced order growth, reflecting a stronger basket mix. The business remained focussed on driving the right kind of order growth through differentiated assortment, stronger consumer relevance and improving value, rather than pursuing structurally weak discount-led growth.

#### **Scaling the Network**

The dark store network continued expanding selectively, with a sharper focus on densification, throughput and assortment capability rather than footprint growth alone.

Instamart added 122 dark stores during the year to reach 1,143 stores across 125+ cities. Active dark store area increased to 4.8 million sq. ft., while average dark store size also increased as the business scaled larger-format stores and megapods to support broader assortment and higher throughput.

As the network matures, the focus remains on sweating existing assets while adding dark stores selectively for coverage and debottlenecking. The current infrastructure can support over 2X the current GOV, alongside further assortment expansion for the primary addressable market.

The integrated Swiggy ecosystem also strengthened user acquisition and retention, as more consumers engaged across Food Delivery, Quick Commerce and Dining Out through Swiggy One.

#### **Only Getting Started**

Quick Commerce in India remains at an early stage, with significant headroom across categories, frequency and wallet share. With industry-leading differentiated assortment, improving basket economics, reliable speed and a scalable network, Instamart remains well-positioned to capture the next phase of category growth as more shopping missions move to quick commerce.
