## **When customers want more than the usual**

### **Instamart: From Grocery App to Everything Store**

Instamart’s evolution gathered pace as non-grocery categories gained traction across electronics, home and kitchen, toys, sports, jewellery, accessories and more. Larger dark stores and megapods enabled broader selection, helping customers use Instamart for more of their household and lifestyle needs.

### **EatRight: Making Healthy Ordering Easier**

EatRight brought health-focused curations such as High Protein, Low Calorie and No Added Sugar into everyday discovery. With 1 in 9 food delivery orders coming through these curations, it showed that healthier eating can scale when made visible, simple and accessible.

### **DeskEats: Solving the Office Food Mission**

DeskEats expanded across 7,000+ tech parks in 30 cities, targeting one of the most repeatable food occasions: the workday meal. It made ordering at work easier, more affordable and better suited to weekday routines.

### **Gourmet: Expanding Premium Food Discovery**

While affordability widened access, Gourmet strengthened the premium end of the platform. By adding more curated restaurant options, it helped Swiggy serve higher-value occasions and balance everyday ordering with indulgent food experiences.

### **Food on Train: Convenience on the Move**

Food on Train expanded to 115+ stations, extending Swiggy’s proposition beyond home, office and restaurant occasions. It brought better food access to travel moments where convenience has traditionally been limited.

### **Toing: Testing Food Delivery for Budget-First Users**

Launched during the year, Toing focuses on price-conscious customers such as students and early-jobbers. It allows us to test a different marketplace model for low-AOV meals, while continuing to expand affordability within the core app through 99 Store and Crazy Deals.

### **Crew: Extending Convenience into Everyday Assistance**

Another platform innovation during the year was the launch of Crew, a personalised concierge-led offering for everyday assistance. From errands and bookings to planning support, it helps us test new ways to serve high-intent customer moments beyond ordering food, grocery or dining out.
