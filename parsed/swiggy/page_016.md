### **Financial Profile**

## **Sharper Economics. Stronger Foundation.**

**Growth at Swiggy has never been about scale alone. As our platform expanded, FY 2025-26 was equally about building a business that grows stronger with scale. We sharpened operating discipline, improved monetisation across the ecosystem and strengthened our balance sheet to support long-term growth through evolving market cycles.**

#### **Scaling with Sharper Operating Discipline**

Across the platform, we continued to improve structural profitability while investing behind growth opportunities and customer experience.

In Food Delivery, stronger operating leverage, rising advertising revenues and improved fleet utilisation supported margin expansion during the year. New propositions such as Bolt and 99 Store also gained meaningful traction by addressing distinct consumer needs around speed and affordability while supporting healthier delivery economics.

In Quick Commerce, the focus remained on improving order quality, increasing wallet share and driving larger baskets instead of pursuing unsustainable discount-led growth. Megapods enabled deeper assortment expansion, while initiatives such as Maxxsaver supported larger carts, stronger wallet consolidation and improving contribution margins. The growing mix of non-grocery categories further strengthened engagement across use cases.

We also adopted a calibrated approach towards network expansion. Dark store additions increasingly prioritised utilisation, assortment depth and hyperlocal density rather than aggressive footprint growth alone. Our existing network today has the capacity to support more than 2x the current order base, enabling greater focus on sweating assets, improving utilisation and selectively expanding where required.

Alongside this, we remained disciplined on user quality and long-term economics, with increasing emphasis on higher-value retained cohorts, wallet share and network productivity metrics.

#### **Expanding Monetisation Beyond Transactions**

As engagement across the platform deepened, we continued to strengthen monetisation beyond core transactions.

Advertising revenues scaled further across our ecosystem as more restaurant, merchant and brand partners leveraged the platform for visibility, discovery and customer acquisition.

Swiggy One continued to strengthen retention and cross-platform engagement by improving affordability and convenience across food delivery, quick commerce and dining out. More than 35% of users were utilising multiple services on the platform by the end of the year, reflecting the growing strength of our integrated ecosystem.

Our integrated operating model also enabled us to unlock additional monetisation opportunities through adjacent offerings such as Dineout, Scenes and newer platform innovations, while leveraging a common technology stack, fulfilment network and user base across businesses.

#### **Strengthening the Balance Sheet for the Next Phase of Growth**

During the year, we further strengthened our balance sheet, giving us the capital depth and flexibility to pursue long-term growth.

We successfully completed an INR 10,000 Crores Qualified Institutional Placement, one of the largest non-BFSI QIPs in India. The issue saw strong participation from domestic and global institutional investors, reflecting confidence in our business fundamentals, execution capability and long-term opportunity.

Alongside this, the monetisation of our stake in Rapido unlocked approximately INR 2,400 Crores, further enhancing liquidity and financial flexibility.

Together, these actions have reinforced our ability to invest behind growth, innovation, infrastructure and customer propositions while maintaining disciplined fiscal prudence. In a dynamic and competitive hyperlocal commerce landscape, a stronger capital position gives us the confidence to execute consistently, stay agile and pursue our strategic priorities without making short-term trade-offs.
