### **99 Store: Affordable Meals, Bigger Reach**

99 Store scaled to 500+ cities, making everyday meals more accessible for price-conscious customers. Built around fast-prep, single-serve food, it helped Swiggy address a sharper affordability need without diluting the overall platform experience.

### **Maxxsaver: Making Larger Baskets Make Sense**

Maxxsaver encouraged customers to build fuller Instamart carts by offering better value on larger baskets. This helped shift behaviour from top-up buying to more planned household purchases, improving both customer value and order economics.

### **Quick India Movement: Value Meets Discovery**

Quick India Movement gave Instamart a high-energy sale format to showcase its expanded assortment. It helped customers discover that quick commerce could serve not just daily essentials, but also festive, seasonal, impulse and non-grocery purchase missions.

### **Swiggy One and Fee-Led Interventions: Reducing Friction**

Through membership benefits, targeted affordability moves and sharper fee structures, we continued improving perceived value for customers. The focus was clear: make repeat usage easier, more rewarding and more relevant across services.
