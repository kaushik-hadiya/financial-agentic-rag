## **Business Strategies**

### **Expand Platform Use-Cases**

Swiggy remains focussed on expanding adjacent offerings across Food Delivery, Quick Commerce and Out-of-Home consumption to increase platform engagement and address a wider set of consumer needs.

### **Strengthen Quick Commerce Network**

Swiggy is focussed on scaling Instamart through expansion of dark-store infrastructure, broader assortment availability and improvements in fulfilment speed and operational efficiency.

### **Invest in Technology and Logistics**

Swiggy will continue investing in technology capabilities, AI-led solutions, automation and logistics infrastructure to improve scalability, operational productivity and customer experience.

### **Deepen Consumer Engagement**

Swiggy aims to strengthen brand visibility and customer retention through targeted marketing, loyalty programmes, content engagement and personalised user experiences.
