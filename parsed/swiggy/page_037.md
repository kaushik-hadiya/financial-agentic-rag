## **Risk Management**

Swiggy recognises that risk management is integral to the Company’s strategy. We believe our success as an organisation depends on our ability to identify and leverage opportunities while managing the risks that accompany them. The board has constituted a Risk Management Committee responsible for overseeing the Company’s risk management framework, reviewing the Company’s risk profile periodically, and ensuring that adequate processes are in place for the identification, assessment, and mitigation of key risks.

Our enterprise risk management framework is embedded within strategic and operational decision-making. Risks are identified at both the Company and individual business levels, assessed on the basis of their potential impact and likelihood, assigned clear ownership, and the effectiveness of mitigation plans is monitored. Governance of risk is organised in line with the Three Lines Model, all of which are under the oversight of the Board and its Committees.

The Enterprise Risk Management team drives risk-management procedures across the organisation, and the framework is reviewed periodically to keep pace with emerging risks and developments in our markets and industry. The policy was last revised in October, 2025 in line with SEBI Listing Regulations. The Updated Risk Management Policy can be accessed at <u>https://www.swiggy.com/corporate/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Swiggy-RMC-Policy-ERM.pdf.</u>

Each principal risk set out below is mapped to the sustainability dimension it most closely relates to – Environmental (E), Social (S) or Governance (G) – and is described together with the way we seek to manage it.

## **Service Quality and Customer Trust**

### **Governance**

Swiggy’s growth rests on consumer trust and the strength of its network of restaurants, delivery partners, sellers, and brand partners. Inconsistent service quality, delays or availability interruptions, errors in fulfilment, or an inconsistent support experience could weaken confidence, reduce engagement, and affect retention across our food delivery, quick commerce, and other offerings.

We invest continuously in product innovation, service reliability and responsive support, including specialised resolution channels and structured training for our consumer- and partner-facing teams. Feedback from consumers and partners is tracked closely and channelled back into product and operational improvements, and we monitor service and satisfaction indicators across each business to sustain a consistent, high-quality experience.

## **Brand and Reputation**

### **Governance**

The strength of the Swiggy brand is central to user acquisition, partner participation and long-term value creation. Commentary or publicity concerning our business model, partner earnings and well-being, technology, privacy, sustainability practices, or financial performance, as well as intellectual property matters, can influence how the brand is perceived and may give rise to remediation costs.

We maintain clear brand and communication guidelines and a dedicated function that monitors public sentiment and responds in a timely and transparent manner. We proactively share the Company’s progress and contributions with stakeholders and communities, while a consumer-insights function engages with stakeholders to inform continuous improvement of our products and services.

## **Competition**

### **Governance**

We operate in highly competitive and rapidly evolving markets. Existing and new competitors may introduce differentiated products, alternative pricing or business models, or invest significantly in promotions and capacity, particularly in the quick commerce segment. Such competition could affect our market position, growth and profitability.

We compete through continuous innovation, a deep understanding of consumer behaviour, and a sustained focus on the experience we offer consumers and partners. We strengthen our position through differentiated products, efficient operations, disciplined investment, and the breadth of our portfolio spanning food delivery, quick commerce, and adjacent services.

## **Ecosystem and Stakeholder Dependence | Social & Governance**

Our operations rely on a large ecosystem of delivery partners, restaurant and seller partners, and third-party providers (i.e. technology, payments, etc.). The availability, engagement and well-being of delivery partners, continuity of supply from partners and the dependability of key third parties are important to service continuity. In addition, the evolving regulatory framework for gig workers may over time influence our operating costs and compliance requirements.

We work closely with each stakeholder group through dedicated support teams, timely grievance resolution, and regular training on service quality, safety and responsible practices. We extend well-being measures to delivery partners, including insurance cover and health and safety initiatives, and we forecast capacity requirements to secure availability and continuity of service. We continue to monitor developments in the regulation of gig workers and engage constructively to adapt our practices accordingly.

## **Technology and Information Security**

### **Governance**

Reliable, secure and scalable technology is fundamental to our operations. System interruptions or capacity constraints could affect availability and the customer experience, while an evolving threat landscape exposes us to cyber incidents such as social engineering, denial-of-service attacks, credential-based attacks, and malware. A significant incident could disrupt services, affect data, and carry financial and reputational risks.

We operate dedicated security teams and defined frameworks, supported by advanced security tooling, regular testing & maintenance, resilience, and business continuity planning. We run bug-bounty programmes and internal initiatives such as hackathons to identify and address vulnerabilities, conduct periodic security assessments, and sustain a strong security culture through ongoing employee awareness and training.

## **Data Privacy and Responsible AI**

### **Governance**

As a platform that processes personal data at scale, we are responsible for protecting the data and for complying with an evolving data-protection and privacy regime, including India’s data-protection law. A shortfall in safeguarding personal data or in meeting applicable obligations could lead to regulatory, financial and trust-related consequences.

We are also expanding our use of artificial intelligence across several business operations. While these technologies create meaningful opportunities to improve experience and efficiency, they also introduce considerations relating to the accuracy and reliability of outputs, fairness, transparency, the responsible use of data, dependence on third-party models, and a developing regulatory landscape. The same technologies can also be used by external parties to increase the sophistication of cyber and fraud-related activity, which we factor into our defences.

We follow privacy-by-design principles, supported by data governance and privacy protocols, so that personal data is processed lawfully, securely, and transparently, and we continue to align our practices with applicable requirements. For artificial intelligence, we are strengthening governance for responsible development and use – including appropriate human oversight, review of model performance and outcomes, and controls over data and third-party tools – so that these technologies are deployed safely, ethically and consistently with our values and obligations.

## **Regulatory and Compliance Landscape**

### **Governance**

We operate across food delivery, quick commerce and adjacent services in a dynamic regulatory environment. Changes in laws and regulations, or in their interpretation and enforcement – including those relating to our business model, social security of gig workers, food safety and quality standards, data protection, foreign investment and permitted business models, consumer protection, competition and taxation – could increase our cost of compliance, require adjustments to our business practices, or give rise to claims and operational restrictions.

We maintain a compliance framework and monitor regulatory developments on an ongoing basis. Our legal, compliance and secretarial functions, supported by external advisors where appropriate, work to ensure timely alignment with applicable requirements, and regulatory considerations are embedded within our planning and operational decisions.

## **Macroeconomic and Geopolitical Environment**

### **Social & Governance**

Our performance is influenced by broader macroeconomic and geopolitical conditions, including movements in inflation, interest rates, and currencies, as well as shifts in consumer sentiment and discretionary spending. A slowdown in consumption or wider economic or geopolitical uncertainty could moderate demand, affect order frequency and values, and increase operating costs.

Our diversified portfolio across food delivery, quick commerce, and adjacent services – spanning a range of customer needs, price points, and use cases, including everyday essentials – helps moderate the effects of economic cycles. We monitor macroeconomic and market trends on an ongoing basis and adapt our product, pricing, and resource allocation decisions accordingly, with a continued emphasis on efficiency and resilience.

## **Talent and Organisation**

### **Social**

Our ability to attract, develop, and retain talented people, together with the strength of our leadership pipeline and succession planning, is important to our ability to grow, innovate, and develop technologically. Difficulty in securing or retaining critical skills could affect our execution and competitiveness.

We invest in our people through structured learning, leadership development, and career growth opportunities, including capability building in emerging areas. We support well-being through flexible policies and wellness initiatives, foster an inclusive and diverse workplace underpinned by fair and transparent performance practices, and listen to our people through regular feedback mechanisms to strengthen engagement and retention.
