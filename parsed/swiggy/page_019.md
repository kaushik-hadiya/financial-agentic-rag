### **Food Delivery**

## **More Meals.**
## **More Moments.**

**FY2025-26 was a year of strong growth and improving profitability for Food Delivery, as we strengthened consumer engagement by unlocking newer consumption moments.**

#### **The Year that Was**

The business delivered strong momentum, with nearly 20% YoY GOV growth. We added 2.8 million MTUs during the year, while order growth improved to mid-teens from single digits in the previous year.

Cross-pollination across the Swiggy ecosystem strengthened user growth, retention and frequency, with more consumers engaging across multiple services. Profitability also improved, with Contribution margins expanding to ~7.5%, while Adjusted EBITDA crossed INR 1,000 Crores, nearly doubling from the previous year. Adjusted EBITDA margins reached ~2.9% of GOV, driven by advertising, better fleet utilisation and operating leverage.

#### **Built for Every Food Mission**

Growth during the year was driven by propositions built around distinct consumer need states across speed, value, accessibility and premiumisation.

Bolt and One BLCK strengthened the speed-led proposition, with Bolt continuing to contribute more than 1 in 10 Food Delivery orders while opening up new consumption occasions across desserts, beverages, snacks and other fast-prep meals. Together, Bolt and 99 Store now account for around one-fifth of Food Delivery platform volumes.

The platform also expanded relevance across evolving food habits and routines. EatRight curation including High Protein, Low Calorie and No Added Sugar now contribute nearly 1 in 9 platform orders, reflecting rising demand for healthier food choices. DeskEats strengthened office food missions across tech parks in 30+ cities, while Food on Train expanded delivery access across railway-led travel occasions.

At the premium end, Gourmet continued adding higher-value restaurant partners and balancing affordability-led growth with premiumisation-led AOV expansion.

#### **Speed and Affordability**

Affordability remained one of the biggest unlocks for category expansion during the year. However, the focus remained on building affordability sustainably rather than through structurally weak economics.

99 Store helped make ordering more accessible for value-conscious users while also driving higher-frequency behaviour. Propositions such as Bolt were designed around shorter delivery radii capabilities and fast-prep formats, helping optimise delivery economics while widening category participation.

Even as affordability initiatives expanded, overall user charges remained stable, while platform AOVs continued to stay healthy through premiumisation and broader consumption occasions. Swiggy One further strengthened user retention and repeat behaviour by deepening the connected membership ecosystem across Food Delivery, Quick Commerce and Dining Out.

#### **Doing More with Every Order**

Advertising continued emerging as an important profitability lever during the year, with restaurant partners increasingly adopting self-serve discovery and visibility tools on the platform.

At the same time, improving fleet utilisation, stronger order density and operating leverage across fulfilment costs supported margin expansion even as the business continued investing behind newer propositions and affordability-led formats.

The combination of stronger user growth, rising monetisation and sharper operational efficiency continued strengthening the structural profitability profile of the business.

#### **Miles to Go**

Food Delivery in India is significantly underpenetrated relative to global markets, with meaningful headroom for higher frequency and broader adoption. As ordering habits expand beyond traditional meal occasions, we believe the next phase of growth will come from making Food Delivery more deeply embedded into everyday life across convenience-led, health-led, value-led and impulse consumption moments.
