### **Out-of-Home Consumption**

## **Expanding Occasions Beyond the Home**

**Out-of-Home Consumption quietly became one of our strongest turnaround stories, delivering its first full year of profitability. The segment has evolved from an integrated acquisition into a high-growth platform that expands our role across the consumer’s food journey.**

photo: Dineout restaurant interior

### **The Year that Was**

The business delivered strong GOV growth of 50.6% YoY to INR 4,645 Crores, supported by rising consumer adoption, growing restaurant participation and a sharper dining-out proposition. Profitability improved through the year, with Adjusted EBITDA margin reaching 0.6% of GOV, supported by growing advertising revenue, low fulfilment costs, platform integration and operating leverage.

Dineout has been a success story since acquisition and broke even in early 2025, within two years of integration into the Swiggy app, reflecting the strength of our acquisition-led integration playbook.

#### **Dineout at the Centre**

Dineout remained the core of this segment, helping consumers discover restaurants, make reservations and complete payments through the Swiggy app. It has filled a clear whitespace in our hyperlocal portfolio and complemented our core Food Delivery business by extending our presence from ordering in to eating out.

For restaurants, it is increasingly becoming a strategic growth lever. Curated selections, social discovery, dining rewards and advertising solutions are helping partners improve visibility, drive footfall and encourage repeat visits.

#### **Beyond Delivery**

Dining out opens up a different food consumption opportunity, especially across premium restaurants, social occasions and higher discretionary spending.

As of FY 2025-26, the segment had crossed 47k Average Monthly Active Restaurants, growing over 30% year-on-year. Many of these partners also operate on Food Delivery, creating a stronger discovery and retention flywheel across the Swiggy ecosystem. A meaningful share of new Dineout partners are premium dining restaurants, expanding access to a different food consumption opportunity and an incremental profit pool.

#### **Experiences in the Making**

Scenes remained nascent but added a new layer to our Out-of-Home proposition, allowing us to test curated events and hyperlocal experiences around dining, entertainment and social occasions. From live music to restaurant-led events, it brings discovery and booking into the same Swiggy journey, helping turn dining out into a fuller experience. As it evolves, Scenes gives us another way to deepen engagement, support restaurant partners and participate in more social occasions beyond the meal.

#### **A Larger Seat at the Table**

Dining out remains underpenetrated and has the potential to grow faster than Food Delivery over the medium term. With a growing partner base, premium dining expansion, inherently larger AOVs, low fulfilment costs and improving advertising monetisation, Dineout’s profitability is poised to strengthen further as the business scales.
