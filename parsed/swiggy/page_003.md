## **Bringing India Closer to Joy**

**Joy is a craving answered. The missing ingredient at the right time. A last-minute table for dinner. A quick bite between meetings. A thoughtful gift across the city. A task handled before the day gets away.**

Swiggy shortens the distance between wanting and having, planning and experiencing, desire and delight.

Our platform now powers many missions, creating millions of moments that bring India closer:

**Closer to the food people love.**
**Closer to products that make daily life easier.**
**Closer to experiences worth sharing.**
**Closer to opportunities for every partner.**

Technology helps us understand what each moment needs. Our network helps us respond with speed, choice, affordability and reliability. This allows us to serve more occasions, make convenience feel effortless and bring joy within reach. Joy should travel beyond the order. Its impact should be felt in more livelihoods, greater inclusion, thriving communities and a lighter footprint on the planet. As our role in everyday life deepens, we remain focused on improving safety and well-being, reducing waste and emissions and building responsibly.
