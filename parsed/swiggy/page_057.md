## **Corporate Governance Report**

**Annexure-3**

### **1. COMPANY’S PHILOSOPHY ON CODE OF GOVERNANCE:**

Swiggy Limited believes that sound corporate governance is fundamental to sustainable growth, responsible innovation and long-term value creation. Its governance framework is built on the core principles of integrity, transparency, accountability, fairness and ethical business conduct, which guide all decisions and actions across the organisation.

The Company’s governance philosophy is fundamentally centered on building and managing sustainable, growing businesses with an unwavering commitment to honesty, transparency and accountability. Governance is viewed not merely as a regulatory obligation, but as a strategic imperative that strengthens stakeholder trust and enhances long-term shareholder value.

The Company conducts its affairs in compliance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and other applicable laws and regulations. It has instituted robust internal controls, comprehensive policies and a structured risk management framework to ensure responsible decision-making and effective oversight.

Recognizing shareholders’ inherent right to be informed about the Company’s performance, their interests are safeguarded with a deep sense of responsibility and stewardship. The Company consistently provides comprehensive, timely and accurate information on matters affecting its business and financial results, thereby facilitating informed decision-making and reinforcing investor confidence.

The Board of Directors plays a pivotal role in upholding high standards of governance by providing strategic guidance, monitoring management performance and ensuring alignment with the Company’s long-term objectives. The Company remains committed to maximizing stakeholder value – encompassing customers, restaurant and merchant partners, delivery executives, employees and shareholders – while fostering a culture of ethics, compliance and transparency across its entire ecosystem.

At its core, the Company’s corporate governance approach reflects a steadfast commitment to business excellence, sustainable performance and the creation of enduring value for all stakeholders.

The Company further recognises that Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) considerations are integral to its long-term business strategy. The Board oversees ESG-related risks and opportunities – including cybersecurity governance, data privacy, supply chain sustainability, and the welfare of its delivery partner ecosystem. As one of the top 1,000 listed entities by market capitalisation, the Company is committed to publishing its Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR) as part of the Annual Report, in accordance with Regulation 34(2)(f) of the Listing Regulations.

### **2. BOARD OF DIRECTORS:**

**a)** The Board comprises of members having varied skills, experience and knowledge. The Board has an optimum combination of both Independent and Non-Independent Directors. The Board is constituted in accordance with the requirements of Regulation 17 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

As on March 31, 2026, the Board of Directors consists of 8 Directors viz., comprising of four Independent and four Non-Independent Directors, out of the Independent Directors, one is woman Director, and one is Chairman of the Board. Out of the four Non-Independent Directors, one is Managing Director and Group Chief Executive Officer, one is a Whole-Time Director – Head of Innovation, and two are Non-Executive Nominee Directors which is in conformity with the requirements of Regulation 17 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (Listing Regulations). The Chairman of the Board is a Non-Executive and Independent Director. 50% of the Board Members are Independent Directors and the rest 50% consists of executive directors and nominee directors.

**b)** During FY2025-26, none of our Directors acted as Member in more than 10 committees or as Chairperson in more than 5 committees across all listed entities where they serve as a Director. For the purpose of determination of limit of the Board Committees, chairpersonship and membership of the Audit Committee and Stakeholders’ Relationship Committee has been considered as per Regulation 26(1)(b) of SEBI Listing Regulations. Further, there are no inter se relationships between our Board Members.

**c)** Except for the Chairman and Independent Directors, the remaining two are Non-Independent, Non-Executive Directors (Nominee Directors) and two are Non-Independent, Executive Director who are liable to retire by rotation as per Section 152(6) of the Companies Act, 2013.
