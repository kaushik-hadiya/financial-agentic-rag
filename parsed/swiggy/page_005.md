### **About Swiggy**

## **Delivering Convenience that Keeps Pace with India**

**From midnight hunger pangs and office lunches to festive orders and forgotten essentials, we power millions of everyday decisions through a platform designed to make convenience faster, simpler, smarter and effortless.**

Swiggy has grown into one of India's leading convenience platforms, serving the changing needs of urban consumers. Through a unified app and integrated ecosystem, we bring together food delivery, quick commerce, dining out, events and emerging hyperlocal services into one seamless experience.

What makes our platform distinctive is not just the breadth of services, but how they work together. A shared technology backbone, fulfilment network, membership ecosystem and partner base enable us to serve multiple everyday use cases while continuously improving speed, selection, affordability and reliability. As consumer expectations evolve, we continue to expand the role we play in everyday life through customer-backward innovation, deeper personalisation and high-frequency engagement across categories.

At the same time, we remain focussed on building responsibly. Across our platform, we continue to strengthen initiatives around delivery partner welfare, sustainability, governance, responsible sourcing and community impact, while building a business designed for long-term resilience and inclusive growth.
