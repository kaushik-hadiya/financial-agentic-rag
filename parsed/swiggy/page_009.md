### **Instamart: From Essentials to Everyday Convenience**

Quick Commerce remained one of the most dynamic and competitive spaces in Indian retail. Instamart’s GOV grew 94.1% to INR 28,496 Crores, while Net Order Value (NOV) grew 69.9% to INR 20,210 Crores. Orders increased 44.4% to 412 million. AOV rose from INR 514 to INR 691, supported by broader assortment, a higher non-grocery mix and larger-basket buying behaviour. Average MTUs grew 73.7% to 12.3 million.

Our strategy was not to pursue volume at any cost. We focussed on wallet share, basket quality, availability and operating leverage. Contribution margin improved to (2.8%) of GOV from (4.0%) in the previous year, led by higher AOVs, improved monetisation, better infrastructure utilisation and lower dependence on unsustainable incentives.

The Quick India Movement was an important milestone in communicating the new Instamart proposition. It helped users see Instamart not only as a grocery and essentials platform, but as a broader “everything store” across categories and occasions.

We also took a more deliberate approach to network expansion. After front-loading investments in dark stores, our focus shifted towards sweating existing assets, improving utilisation and adding stores in a calibrated manner for coverage and debottlenecking. By year-end, Instamart had 1,143 active dark stores, up from 1,021, and total active dark store area of 4.8 million sq. ft. The infrastructure built can support significantly higher throughput as utilisation improves, alongside further assortment expansion for the primary addressable market.

The category will continue to attract capital and competition. Our answer is to build a business that wins through a distinctive product offering, not only through price. Quick delivery is increasingly becoming table stakes. The right to win will come from reliable availability, breadth of assortment, differentiated categories, strong partner brands and the ability to make Instamart a destination for everyday upgrades, not just everyday essentials. Early efforts such as Noice, our clean-food private brand, are part of this direction.

The business delivered its first full year of profitability, with Adjusted EBITDA of INR 29 Crores and margin of 0.6% of GOV. Average monthly active restaurants grew 30.2% to 46.6k.

#### **Platform Innovations: Testing the Next Set of Use Cases**

Platform Innovations exists as a deliberate sandbox for experiments that test new consumer propositions and business models. The idea is not to optimise only what exists, but to challenge existing paradigms where we see a large unmet need.

During the year, Toing was launched as an affordability-led experiment for budget-conscious consumers through a restaurant marketplace model. Crew was also introduced as part of our effort to explore newer convenience-led services. Not every experiment will scale at the same pace, but each helps us understand where our technology stack, fulfilment capability and consumer relationship can create future opportunities.

#### **Capital Strength and the Ability to Execute**

One of the key developments during the year was the strengthening of our balance sheet. We completed an INR 10,000 Crores Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP), the second-largest ever in India outside the Banking, Financial Services and Insurance (BFSI) sector. The issue saw more than 4x demand from domestic and global investors, even amidst market volatility. Alongside the INR 2,400 Crores unlock from the Rapido stake sale, this gave us a much stronger capital base and a clear strategic advantage in highly competitive categories.

This matters because our categories are large, attractive and continue to evolve. It allows us to invest behind fulfilment infrastructure, technology, product, brand, assortment and customer experience with a long-term orientation. It also gives us the confidence to make choices that are right for the business, even if they create temporary short-term trade-offs.

As Swiggy scales, we remain conscious that the platform is built on the strength of a large ecosystem. We remain focused on making our platform more inclusive and responsible. During the year, we continued to advance this agenda through consistent, measurable action. While there is more to do, our approach is clear: embed sustainability into the way we operate, innovate and grow.
